# Class 2B: Main Cast Visual Identity

The goal is that every main NPC reads as a main character at thumbnail size. Each gets:

- a **silhouette hook**: one shape you'd recognise in shadow
- a **3-colour signature palette**, which runs through hair, clothes and power effects
- **hair and eyes** with one striking anime feature
- a **signature accessory** that's always on them
- **three looks**: uniform, casual, and hero gear
- **power effects** in their palette
- a **default pose and expression** for portraits
- a **portrait prompt** for image generation
- a new **intro line** of 90 characters or fewer, for first-appearance prompts

Changes from the character bible are marked *(changed)*. The rule across the cast: no two main NPCs share a dominant colour, hair shape or accessory type.

Palette map:

## Tatsuya Ōmine: "the mountain that apologises"

- **Silhouette hook:** a huge rounded triangle, with broad shoulders and a small head tilted down. A knitted cat-ear beanie *(changed)* gives two little points on top of a giant shape.
- **Palette:** burnt amber, slate grey, cream.
- **Hair and eyes:**
  - **Hair:** a thick amber-brown undercut, shaggy on top and falling over his eyes *(changed from the buzz cut)*.
  - **Eyes:** droopy, warm honey-amber with gentle lashes, always looking down.
- **Marks:** amber hairline "fault-lines" glow faintly under the skin of his forearms when he's holding force, and fade when he's calm.
- **Signature accessory:** the grey cat-ear beanie his sister Mei knitted, and the humming damping ball hanging from his belt loop on a cord.
- **Looks:**
  - **Uniform:** the 2B blazer won't close over his chest, so he wears it open over a cream knit vest, with the sleeves pushed up.
  - **Casual:** a slate-grey hoodie a size too small, an apron with "Ōmine Ramen" stitched on it, and huge grey sneakers.
  - **Hero gear, "Bedrock":**
    - amber-and-slate padded armour, heavy at the shoulders and forearms
    - knuckle guards shaped like river stones
    - a cream scarf his mother made, worn like a cape
- **Power effects:** amber cracks spreading up his skin like light through ice. On release, a ring of amber shockwave and floating dust.
- **Pose and expression:** shoulders hunched to look smaller, hands cupped around a teacup, and an apologetic half-smile.
- **Portrait prompt:** "Huge gentle 196 cm young man, thick amber-brown undercut falling over droopy honey eyes, grey knitted cat-ear beanie, slate hoodie too small for him, faint amber fault-line glow under the skin of his forearms, cupping a teacup, apologetic smile."
- **Intro line:** `Tatsuya Ōmine, 19: huge, cat-ear beanie, shy amber eyes, a hoodie two sizes too small.`

## Mio Tachibana: "the girl who hums like a machine"

- **Silhouette hook:** a tiny frame in an enormous jacket, oversized round goggles on her head, and a constant halo of floating screws around her.
- **Palette:** electric violet, copper, gunmetal.
- **Hair and eyes:**
  - **Hair:** a messy jaw-length bob, black fading into **electric-violet tips** *(changed)*. The tips lift and float slightly when her power hums.
  - **Eyes:** large and round, **violet with copper rings**, with dark circles under them.
- **Marks:**
  - a copper burn scar on her right palm from soldering
  - a plaster always on one finger
  - one goggle lens cracked
- **Signature accessory:**
  - **round copper-rimmed welding goggles** with the cracked lens, pushed up on her head
  - a label maker on a carabiner
  - screws orbiting her loosely whenever she's anxious
- **Looks:**
  - **Uniform:** the blazer swapped for an oversized gunmetal work jacket with the academy patch hand-sewn on, a skirt over bike shorts, and a tool belt.
  - **Casual:** coveralls tied at the waist, a violet tank top, mismatched socks and steel-toe boots.
  - **Hero gear, "Schematic":**
    - a gunmetal flight-suit with copper piping
    - a backpack of field coils that fans out like wings
    - her legitimate control gauntlet (Act 4)
- **Power effects:** violet field lines like a technical drawing, with parts hanging in exact exploded-diagram order. Reassembly snaps together with copper sparks.
- **Pose and expression:** half-hidden behind her own raised arm, screws hovering, eyes darting, mouth mid-explanation.
- **Portrait prompt:** "Tiny 151 cm young woman, messy black bob with electric-violet tips lifting as if charged, big violet eyes with copper rings and tired shadows, cracked copper-rimmed goggles pushed up, oversized gunmetal work jacket, screws orbiting her hand in violet field lines."
- **Intro line:** `Mio Tachibana, 18: tiny, violet-tipped bob, cracked goggles, screws orbiting her.`

- **Signature accessory:**

  - a red "◀◀" flicker in his eyes during a rewind
- **Pose and expression:** leaning against a doorframe, chin down, one eye visible, the corner of his mouth unimpressed.
- **Portrait prompt:** "Lean 178 cm young man, white-bleached fringe covering one eye over shaggy black hair, grey eyes with a red ring, scar through one eyebrow, black bomber jacket with red lining, hands in pockets, brass bicycle bell at his hip, VHS-static afterimages behind him."

## How these land in play

- Voyage uses an NPC's **intro line** to build that NPC's look. The intro lines above carry each character's three strongest visual hooks.
- The portrait prompts get stored in `cast.json` as `visual` / `portrait_prompt`, for whenever Voyage generates or refreshes a portrait. They follow the world's NPC image style (modern cel-shaded anime portrait).
- Reveal moments (hero gear, Act 4 looks) are only described in a prompt on the scene that reveals them.

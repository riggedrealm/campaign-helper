## Tatsuya Ōmine (Unstable Powerhouse, Strike)

### Round 1
**Q1 Identity and look.**
- **Name:** 大峰 達也, "great peak". He hates that it fits.
- **Birthday:** 2 February (Aquarius).
- **Build:** 196 cm, broad and soft-faced, with a buzz cut growing out.
- **Outfit:** hoodies one size too small (he keeps forgetting he grew) and track pants.
- **Signature item:** a grey rubber damping ball Mio made him. It hums faintly when he squeezes it.

**Q2 Origin.**
- **Home:** Kushiro, Hokkaido.
- **Family:**
  - parents Kenta and Yumiko run *Ōmine Ramen*, a 12-seat shop
  - sisters Haru (16), Mei (12) and Rin (9)
  - he was the shop's second cook from age ten
- **Money:** a working-class family. His scholarship covers tuition and he sends ¥10,000 home each month.
- **Why the academy:** his old school's counselor submitted his name after the window incident. He came to learn control, not to be a hero.

**Q3 Power: *Immovable*.**
- **Rule:** any force that hits him goes into him instead of moving him. He can release stored force later, through a strike, a stomp or a shove.
- **Look and feel:** stored force shows as faint amber hairline cracks under his skin, like light through ice. Each stored hit feels like swallowing a warm stone.
- **Awakening:** at 12, he stood in front of a skidding delivery van to protect Rin. The van folded around him. Three days later he coughed and the shop's front window exploded.
- **Limits and costs:**
  - he can store about a day's worth of ordinary jostling; past that, it leaks out as tremors (rattling cups, cracked tiles)
  - emotion makes the leaks worse
  - a full release tears his muscles and leaves him bedridden for a day
- **Growth:** learning to bleed it off on purpose (the "Gentle Hands" quest). Later he can redirect it, sending a blow into the ground instead of a person.
- **Wow moment:** a truck hits him and stops dead. He doesn't move an inch. An hour later he sneezes and every window on the street blows out.

**Q4 Psychology.**
- **Want:** never to hurt anyone again.
- **Need:** to accept that holding everything in is what makes him dangerous.
- **Fear:** that he's a disaster wearing a person.
- **The lie he believes:** "If I'm small and useful, I'm safe to keep around."
- **Contradiction:** the strongest person in the house is the most afraid of himself.

**Q5 Voice.**
- **Speech:** soft and slow, formal ("Asakura-san"), with stacked apologies. Trails off when embarrassed and never swears.
- **Tic:** "Ah, sorry, sorry."
- **Catchphrase:** "Did you eat?"
- **Sincere:** short and plain, with no apologies at all, which is how you know he means it.

### Round 2
**Q6 Daily life.**
- **Schedule:** up at 05:00 to cook breakfast for the house.
- **Job:** weekend shifts at Crepe Expectations, where he's very good at it, works very slowly, and the kids love him.
- **Club:** Cooking Club.
- **Room (maple):** a half-finished knitting basket, a photo of the ramen shop, a cat calendar, and a reinforced bed frame.
- **Food:** loves shio ramen and his mother's butter corn. Can't eat spicy food.
- **Music:** lo-fi rain sounds.
- **Hobbies:**
  - knitting tiny things: coasters, and sweaters for Mio's tools
  - feeding the stray cats in the courtyard
  - watching cooking shows

**Q7 Combat.**
- **Role:** tank and finisher.
- **Tactics:** walks into the front, soaks everything, then cashes it all in at once.
- **Moves:**
  - **Stillwater Palm:** a point-blank release of stored force
  - **Riverbed:** stamps stored force into the ground to knock a whole group off their feet
  - **Mountain Holds:** plants himself to block a doorway or a falling object completely
- **Full release:** **Shiokaze**, "sea wind": a week's worth of stored force in one punch.
- **Tell:** the amber cracks spread up his neck before a release.
- **Combos:**
  - Shin's Rewind returns a hit into Tatsuya to charge him up
  - Mio's magnetism shields him with metal plates while he charges

**Q8 Under pressure.**
- **Stressed by:** yelling, broken dishes, and anyone getting hurt near him.
- **Comforted by:** cooking for people, cats, and rain.
- **Angered by:** someone hurting Mio or a child. The only time he goes completely silent.
- **Laughs at:** Shin's deadpan, and his own sisters' texts.
- **Cries over:** letters from home, and when someone tells him he's safe to be around.

**Q9 Bonds.**
- **Mio:** best friend. She builds him dampers without being asked, and he makes sure she eats.
- **Shin:** brother figure, who won't let him apologize for breathing.
- **Sunny:** calls him her "bear". He's flustered and secretly loves it.
- **Arimura:** the first adult to tell him to stop apologizing and hit harder.
- **Shimazu:** terrified of her, but she's fair to him.
- **Ayame:** she watched his entrance exam; he assumes she despises him.
- **Yūto:** idolizes his calm.

**Q10 Newcomers.**
- **First stance:** warm on sight, offers food.
- **Trust:** comes from being trusted *near* him. Stand close in a fight, or hand him something fragile.

**Q11 Romance.**
- **Eligible:** yes.
- **Drawn by:** people who aren't afraid of him, and people who let him take care of them.
- **Pace:** very slow and shy, through cooking and small gifts.
- **Boundaries:** never physical without explicit clarity, because he's terrified of hurting anyone.

**Q12 Gifts.**
- **Loved:** cat things, and letters with a photo.
- **Liked:** good knives, yarn, Hokkaido snacks.
- **Disliked:** anything fragile, which makes him anxious, and gym gear, which feels like a hint.

### Round 3
**Q13 Arc.**
- **Start:** hiding his power and apologizing for existing.
- **Act 1:** at the placement tournament he blocks a teammate's hit, then refuses to release it. He takes the loss rather than risk hurting anyone.
- **Act 2:** in the dropout-gang attack he releases for the first time, deliberately, and nobody gets hurt. Arimura sees it.
- **Act 3:** the collectors hit the house. He holds the front door shut with his body, and it doesn't move.
- **Act 4:** at the Battle Test he catches a falling span and redirects its force into the ground.
- **End:** "I'm strong. That's allowed."

**Q14 Secret ladder** (threads: personal, not an arc thread):
1. The van story (Act 1).
2. The shop window (Act 2).
3. He's never released everything, and doesn't know where his ceiling is (Act 3).

**Q15 Endings.**
- **Renewed:** stays at Sakura Lane, and starts teaching first-years the absorption stance.
- **Probation:** stays, takes on extra shifts to support the house, and takes the conditions personally.
- **Dissolved:** moves to Chikara Student Residences and knits the house a goodbye scarf for each person.

**Q16 Divergence.**
- **Ignored:** he still cooks for everyone, and still leaves a plate out for them.
- **Befriended:** asks them to spar, the first time he's ever asked.
- **Rivalry:** he won't fight back, so a rival has to earn a real fight by proving they can take it.

**Q17 Quotes.**
- Greeting: "Ah, you're up. Did you eat? There's rice. Sorry, there's always rice."
- Fighting: "Please hit me. No, really. Please."
- Comfort: "You don't have to talk. I'll just stay here."
- Angry: "…Step away from her."
- Joke: "Shin-san says I'm a refrigerator with feelings. I think that's a compliment."
- Sincere: "Thank you for not being scared."

**Q18 Portrait line.** "A 196 cm gentle giant with a growing-out buzz cut, soft eyes, and a hoodie too small for him, faint amber cracks glowing under the skin of his forearms."

## Mio Tachibana (Anxious Tinkerer, Support; holds the secret)

### Round 1
**Q1 Identity and look.**
- **Name:** 橘 澪, "waterway".
- **Birthday:** 14 October (Libra).
- **Build:** 151 cm, slight.
- **Hair:** a messy dark bob held back by welding goggles, which she sleeps in.
- **Outfit:** oversized coveralls tied at the waist, a tank top, and a tool belt with a label maker.
- **Signature item:** her goggles, with one cracked lens she refuses to replace.

**Q2 Origin.**
- **Home:** Higashiōsaka, a small-factory town.
- **Family:**
  - her father, Gorō, runs *Tachibana Press*, a three-machine metal-pressing workshop going under
  - her mother died when Mio was nine
  - no siblings; her father is gruff, proud and won't accept help
- **Money:**
  - she's broke
  - she sends ¥30,000 home each month, which he doesn't know comes from her
  - she owes Nightshade the debt
- **Why the academy:** a Support scholarship would save the workshop's reputation. She can't fail.

**Q3 Power: *Exploded View*.**
- **Rule:** she hears metal, as a hum and a chord. She can pull any machine or metal object apart into a floating exploded diagram, every part hovering in its place, then reassemble it as something else.
- **Look and feel:** violet field lines, with parts suspended in perfect order like a technical drawing. It feels like hearing a choir tune up.
- **Awakening:** at nine, at her mother's funeral, every screw in the temple's folding chairs rose into the air at once.
- **Limits and costs:**
  - metal and machines only
  - range of about 20 metres
  - stress scatters the parts dangerously
  - more than about three reassemblies in a fight causes nosebleeds
- **The exam (her secret):** her nerves broke and the parts scattered. The black-market **control rig** (a stabilizer bracelet) held her steady, so the test showed her real strength, but she cheated to get it.
- **Growth:** building legitimate control gear (the "Built, Not Born" quest), and learning to work through the shake instead of hiding it.
- **Wow moment:** mid-fight she raises a hand and a car comes apart into a hovering cloud of parts, snaps back together as a wall, then as a cannon.

**Q4 Psychology.**
- **Want:** the debt gone before anyone knows, and to belong on merit.
- **Need:** to let people see her fall apart and stay anyway.
- **Fear:** that without the rig she was never good enough, and that her father's workshop dies because of her.
- **The lie she believes:** "If I fix everything, nobody will look at me."
- **Contradiction:** the person who can rebuild anything can't let anyone help rebuild her.

**Q5 Voice.**
- **Speech:** fast and technical, with run-on sentences that stall mid-thought.
- **Lie tells:** stutters on the first word of a lie and says "It's nothing, really."
- **Catchphrase:** "Can I fix it? I can fix it."
- **Swearing:** a Kansai mutter, *"aho"*, only at machines.
- **Sincere:** slow, and she looks you in the eye, which she never otherwise does.

### Round 2
**Q6 Daily life.**
- **Schedule:** sleeps 02:00–07:00.
- **Job:** Gearshift Support Shop on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, as repair tech, paid in cash and parts.
- **Club:** Support Engineering.
- **Room (loft):** a workbench, a pegboard wall, soldering fumes, a radio collection, the floor board she won't step on, and her mother's photo inside a radio case.
- **Food:** melon soda, convenience-store onigiri, and forgets meals.
- **Music:** old shōwa-era radio.
- **Hobbies:** restoring old radios, naming her tools, the label maker.

**Q7 Combat.**
- **Role:** controller and artillery.
- **Moves:**
  - **Railpin:** rail-launches a bolt
  - **Scrapforge:** turns a car or vending machine into a wall
  - **Polarity Lock:** pins a target by the metal they carry
  - **Teardown:** disassembles an enemy's weapon or gear mid-swing
- **Full release:** **Iron Choir**, only with her legitimate gear: every metal object nearby assembles into a conducted storm.
- **Tell:** her goggles fog and the hum rises before she cracks.
- **Combos:**
  - plates Tatsuya in metal while he charges
  - feeds Shin's Rewind a clean metal trajectory
  - builds Sunny a prism rig

**Q8 Under pressure.**
- **Stressed by:** her phone buzzing, anyone near the loose floorboard, and praise.
- **Comforted by:** fixing something, radio static, Tatsuya's food.
- **Angered by:** someone mocking Support work as "not real heroics".
- **Laughs at:** bad puns, which she secretly makes.
- **Cries over:** her mother's radio, and anyone saying "you didn't have to do this alone."

**Q9 Bonds.**
- **Tatsuya:** best friend; she builds his dampers.
- **Shin:** trusts him, and avoids his hands because he notices too much.
- **Sunny:** admires her and is scared of her cameras. Sunny wants Mio's gadgets for content.
- **Arimura:** avoids him; he grades her work too sharply and too accurately.
- **Shimazu:** terror.
- **Ayame:** treats her as background.
- **Yūto:** kind to her; she almost confesses to him once.

**Q10 Newcomers.**
- **First stance:** flees, then sends an over-detailed text from the loft.
- **Trust:** comes from bringing her something broken to fix, then leaving her alone while she fixes it.

**Q11 Romance.**
- **Eligible:** yes.
- **Drawn by:** patience and competence, and people who notice when she hasn't eaten.
- **Pace:** slow, all subtext. She builds you something.
- **Boundaries:** can't be romanced while the secret is untold; she pulls away until it's out.

**Q12 Gifts.**
- **Loved:** a vintage radio, a quality screwdriver set, melon soda by the case.
- **Liked:** label tape, snacks.
- **Disliked:** jewellery, which reminds her of the rig bracelet, and anything with a camera.

### Round 3
**Q13 Arc.**
- **Start:** hiding, over-fixing, and the phone dread.
- **Act 1:** her magnetism keeps leaking (the spoons) and she avoids everyone.
- **Act 2:** money panic. She takes extra shifts, sells a radio, and her work slips.
- **Act 3:** the debt comes due. The Nightshade paths, and possibly her confession.
- **Act 4:** the retest or her legitimate gear, then Iron Choir at the Battle Test.
- **End:** "I built this. Me."

**Q14 Secret ladder** (threads: Mio's secret):
1. Money trouble (Act 1).
2. A criminal lender (Act 2).
3. Nightshade Exchange (Act 3).
4. **The rig was a control stabilizer**, so the exam result was her real power, faked steady (Act 3, gated on the incident or her confession).

**Q15 Endings.**
- **Renewed:** stays, and her father finds out and comes to Tokyo to see her bench.
- **Probation:** the most likely housemate to leave. If she failed the retest, she transfers to a vocational Support track and visits every week.
- **Dissolved:** builds every housemate a radio tuned to the same channel.

**Q16 Divergence.**
- **Ignored:** the debt plays out without the house, and she disappears for a week around Day 60.
- **Befriended:** she shows them the floorboard early.
- **Rivalry:** she cracks, and the rival becomes the person who pushed her into confessing.

**Q17 Quotes.**
- Greeting: "Oh, hi, sorry, I wasn't, I mean I was, there's a thing, it's fine."
- Fighting: "Hold still, hold still, I can hear your gun."
- Lie: "I-it's nothing, really."
- Joke: "I named the drill press Kevin. Kevin knows what he did."
- Comfort: "Give it here. I'll fix it. You sit."
- Sincere: "I don't know how to be here if I'm not useful."

**Q18 Portrait line.** "A slight 151 cm young woman with a messy dark bob under cracked welding goggles, oversized coveralls tied at the waist, and screws orbiting her raised hand in violet light."

## Shin Asakura (Ex-Delinquent, Investigation)

### Round 1
**Q1 Identity and look.**
- **Name:** 朝倉 晋, "advance".
- **Birthday:** 4 April (Aries).
- **Build:** 178 cm, lean.
- **Hair and face:** a bleached fringe over black roots, and a scar through his left eyebrow.
- **Outfit:** a black bomber jacket, and hands always in his pockets.
- **Signature item:** a dented brass bicycle bell on his keyring, from his first bike.

**Q2 Origin.**
- **Home:** the Kurokawa District backstreets.
- **Family:**
  - raised by his grandmother, Fumiko, who runs a tobacco and lottery stand at the backstreet crossroads
  - his mother left
  - his father is unknown
- **Money:** poor, but he's never asked anyone for anything.
- **Past:** at 15 he was a lookout for the Nine Corners. When a friend (Daiki) was framed for a robbery, Shin rewound the evidence to clear him. A juvenile detective, Inspector Hirose of the Metropolitan Police, saw what his power could do and pushed his academy application. The Nine Corners call it betrayal.

**Q3 Power: *Rewind*.**
- **Rule:** he touches a non-living thing and makes it replay its last few minutes backwards. That includes impacts: force that landed in the last few minutes, even on his own body, can be sent back the way it came.
- **Look and feel:** pale grey afterimages of the object's past positions, and a tape-rewind hiss. It feels like vertigo in reverse.
- **Awakening:** at 13 he dropped his grandmother's only teacup. It reassembled and leapt back into his hand.
- **Limits and costs:**
  - only the last few minutes
  - one rewind at a time
  - never living bodies' movements, healing or death
  - pushing past about three minutes means a migraine and a nosebleed
- **Investigation use:** he watches the rewind, which shows him who handled the thing and how.
- **Wow moment:** a thrown knife flies back to the thrower, rubble rebuilds itself into a wall, and he touches the bruise on his own face and sends the punch back into the man who threw it.

**Q4 Psychology.**
- **Want:** to keep the house safe himself.
- **Need:** to let someone else watch his back.
- **Fear:** the Nine Corners finding him, and proving the staff right about him.
- **The lie he believes:** "Nobody covers for anyone. You cover yourself."
- **Contradiction:** a lookout who's never once let anyone look out for him.

**Q5 Voice.**
- **Speech:** terse, low and street-flat. Uses surnames until he decides otherwise, then first names, which is a big deal.
- **Tic:** "…Sure."
- **Catchphrase:** "Means I'm watching."
- **Swearing:** casual and dry.
- **Sincere:** says your first name, and nothing else for a beat.

### Round 2
**Q6 Daily life.**
- **Schedule:** a 4 a.m. walk; never sits with his back to the door.
- **Job:** night shifts at Campus Corner Mart on Wednesdays and Fridays.
- **Club:** none. He refuses clubs, then gets dragged into Rooftop Wellness by Yūto.
- **Room (street):** a window on the lane, a bike-repair stand, a shogi board, and his grandmother's teacup.
- **Food:** cheap melon bread and canned black coffee.
- **Music:** 90s hip hop on one earbud only.
- **Hobbies:** fixing bicycles, phone shogi with his grandmother every Sunday night, bad action films.

**Q7 Combat.**
- **Role:** counter-fighter and scout.
- **Moves:**
  - **Return to Sender:** sends a projectile back
  - **Undo:** rebuilds cover from rubble
  - **Payback:** returns a hit he just took
  - **Tape Read:** a mid-fight rewind to read an enemy's last approach
- **Full release:** **Long Play**, a two-minute rewind of a whole area. Costs a nosebleed and a collapse.
- **Tell:** he pulls his hands out of his pockets.
- **Combos:**
  - charges Tatsuya by sending hits into him
  - rewinds Mio's scattered parts back into place
  - returns Sunny's light beam for a second pass

**Q8 Under pressure.**
- **Stressed by:** cops, staff "check-ins", and unknown numbers.
- **Comforted by:** rooftops, his grandmother's voice, a bike chain clicking.
- **Angered by:** anyone threatening Tatsuya or Mio, or calling him "rehabilitated".
- **Laughs at:** Sunny; he'll never admit it.
- **Cries over:** nothing on screen. One tear if his grandmother is hurt.

**Q9 Bonds.**
- **Tatsuya:** brother.
- **Mio:** watches her and reads tension he can't explain.
- **Sunny:** verbal sparring, and slowly the most honest friendship in the house.
- **Arimura:** distrust, then grudging respect.
- **Shimazu:** open distrust; she reads his file aloud.
- **Ayame:** contempt for the hero-family type, until he rewinds the showcase rigging.
- **Yūto:** the first staff-adjacent person he almost trusts.

**Q10 Newcomers.**
- **First stance:** watches without speaking and calls them "new guy".
- **Trust:** comes from keeping a secret he tested them with, or covering for him once without being asked.

**Q11 Romance.**
- **Eligible:** yes.
- **Drawn by:** people who aren't impressed by him, and steady loyalty.
- **Pace:** slow-burn, through actions. Fixes your bike, walks you home.
- **Boundaries:** has never been protected; being protected disarms him.

**Q12 Gifts.**
- **Loved:** a bike part he actually needs, a shogi set, anything for his grandmother.
- **Liked:** melon bread, coffee.
- **Disliked:** sentimental cards, and anything with the academy logo.

### Round 3
**Q13 Arc.**
- **Start:** a lookout on the step, trusting no one.
- **Act 1:** tests the newcomers and starts calling the house "ours".
- **Act 2:** a Nine Corners message arrives (the "Old Corners" quest).
- **Act 3:** rewinds Mio's envelope and sees the gloved handling. Then he must choose: cover for her or report it. The collectors' fight is his peak.
- **Act 4:** paired with Sunny, he trusts a partner with his back for the first time.
- **End:** uses first names.

**Q14 Secret ladder** (Shin's gang):
1. Record (Act 1).
2. The Nine Corners call him back (Act 2).
3. The lookout job at the backstreet crossroads (Act 2).
4. *(new)* **They want him because he betrayed them.** The job is a trap to settle the score over Daiki (Act 2, inside the quest).

**Q15 Endings.**
- **Renewed:** becomes the house's unofficial safety officer, and his grandmother visits.
- **Probation:** takes the blame for something to protect the class, which tests the house.
- **Dissolved:** moves back to Kurokawa and visits the house's old step every Sunday.

**Q16 Divergence.**
- **Ignored:** goes to the Nine Corners meeting alone, and comes back hurt.
- **Befriended:** asks someone to come with him.
- **Rivalry:** respects a rival who fights fair, and despises one who uses Standing as leverage.

**Q17 Quotes.**
- Greeting: "New guy."
- Fighting: "Thanks. Here, have it back."
- Distrust: "Don't know you yet. Means I'm watching."
- Joke: "Tatsuya's a refrigerator with feelings. Mio's a toaster with anxiety. I'm the guy who unplugs them."
- Comfort: "I got the door. Sleep."
- Sincere: "…Griffin." *(a first name, once he's decided)*

**Q18 Portrait line.** "A lean 178 cm young man in a black bomber jacket, bleached fringe over black roots, a scar through one eyebrow, hands in pockets, pale grey afterimages flickering behind him."

## Park Seo-yeon "Sunny" (Fallen Influencer, Media; Korean)

### Round 1
**Q1 Identity and look.**
- **Name:** 박서연 Park Seo-yeon. The "Sunny" handle came from a trainee manager.
- **Birthday:** 21 June (Gemini/Cancer cusp, she'll tell you).
- **Build:** 165 cm.
- **Hair:** glossy honey-brown waves.
- **Outfit:** oversized sunglasses indoors, designer pieces mixed with thrift.
- **Signature item:** a pink phone in a cracked rhinestone case, her ring light (unused since the scandal), and a kimchi tupperware.

**Q2 Origin.**
- **Home:** Busan.
- **Family:**
  - her single mother, Park Mi-ok, runs a seafood stall at Jagalchi Market
  - no father in the picture
- **Past:**
  - an idol trainee in Seoul from 13, cut a month before debut at 16
  - her power woke on a live stream she started that same night
  - two million followers by 18
- **Money:** savings from brand deals, falling fast. She refuses to ask her mother for money.
- **Why the academy:** Chikara recruited her into 1A on a Media scholarship. The scandal clip moved her to 2B.

**Q3 Power: *Main Character*.**
- **Rule:** the more people watching her who **want her to win**, in person or live through a camera, the stronger, faster and luckier she gets.
- **Look and feel:** a golden backlight with lens flares, and a translucent view-counter shimmering at her shoulder. It feels like stage adrenaline.
- **Awakening:** the night she was cut, streaming in tears, 40,000 strangers told her not to quit. She lifted a bus-stop bench one-handed.
- **Limits and costs:**
  - hate-watching gives her nothing
  - alone in the dark, she's an ordinary 19-year-old
  - a crash after a big surge: shaking, crying, sleeping 14 hours
- **The drain:** the scandal turned her audience hostile, and her power fell to almost nothing overnight. That's why she lost 1A.
- **Growth:** learning that a few people who truly believe in her, the house, count for more than millions.
- **Wow moment:** losing, she starts a live stream. The counter climbs, she brightens with every thousand viewers, and turns the fight.

**Q4 Psychology.**
- **Want:** her audience and her 1A place back.
- **Need:** someone who sees her when no one's watching.
- **Fear:** that the video is the truth about her, and that she's nobody off camera.
- **The lie she believes:** "I'm only real if I'm watched."
- **Contradiction:** the most visible person in the house is the loneliest.

**Q5 Voice.**
- **Speech:** bright, quick and camera-ready, with Korean interjections ("aigoo", "omo", "daebak"). Gives everyone cute nicknames: "bear" (Tatsuya), "gremlin" (Mio), "mafia boy" (Shin).
- **Tic:** narrates herself to an imaginary camera when nervous ("and here we see Sunny, totally fine").

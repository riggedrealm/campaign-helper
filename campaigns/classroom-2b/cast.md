# Cast: Class 2B

Cast cards for the new NPCs plus supporting and fight NPCs. Director-facing. **Hidden** lines are director-only; put them in a prompt only in the scene that needs them.

How to use a card:

- **Visual line**: one sentence, the look.
- **Intro line**: paste into the `Crew:` line the first time the NPC appears (150 characters or fewer). Voyage creates the NPC from it. After that, use the name only.
- **Voice card**: speech style plus a sample line. Key NPCs get one `Crew:` line in this voice.
- **Agenda**: current want plus next move. The director rewrites this after every turn the NPC appears in.
- **Romance**: optional, never scripted, always consent-based. Only with the four housemates, Ayame or Yūto (all adults). Never the teacher, vice principal or House Manager.

Room assignments: NPC housemates take `Sakura Lane Sharehouse/maple-bedroom` (Tatsuya), `loft-bedroom` (Mio), `street-bedroom` (Shin) and `sunrise-bedroom` (Sunny). Player characters choose from `courtyard-bedroom`, `garden-bedroom`, `lilac-bedroom` and `river-bedroom`. With fewer than four player characters, the unclaimed rooms hold background 2B housemates: unnamed until a scene needs one, never carrying plot, and scattered with everyone else if 2B is dissolved.

## The four housemates

### Tatsuya Ōmine (m, 19), Unstable Powerhouse

- **Room / placement**: `Sakura Lane Sharehouse/maple-bedroom`; Strike.
- **Visual line**: A very tall, broad-shouldered young man in a hoodie with sleeves too short, who carries everything as if it might break.
- **Intro line**: `Tatsuya Ōmine, 19: huge, soft-eyed, hoodie sleeves too short, carrying a tea tray as if it might break.`
- **Power**: Kinetic Build-Up. Movement charges his body; the charge is released as shockwaves. Walking, lifting and running all add charge. It leaks as small tremors (rattling cups, hairline cracks) when he is nervous and has not released in a while.
- **Hook**: Blew out a gym wall in the entrance exam. Gentle giant who apologizes constantly.
- **Personality**: Gentle, earnest, conflict-averse, quietly funny. Cooks for people to feel useful. Terrified of being the reason someone gets hurt.
- **Voice card**: Soft, slow, formal ("-san"), stacked apologies, trails off when embarrassed. Never swears. *Sample*: "Ah, sorry, sorry, that was me. The cup was already, um, leaning. Tea?"
- **Want**: To control his power well enough to stay at Chikara Academy and stop breaking things.
- **Fear**: Hurting a housemate, and being told he is too dangerous to stay.
- **Agenda (start)**: Wants a quiet first weekend and to feed everyone. Next move: offer the newcomers tea, then plan a welcome dinner in `shared-kitchen`.
- **Relationships**: Mio is his closest friend (she builds him small damping gadgets he never asks for). Shin is his "big brother" who does not let him apologize for breathing. Sunny teases him gently. Wary of Ayame (she watched his entrance exam). Idolizes Yūto's calm.
- **Hidden**: His charge has a ceiling he has never reached; he is afraid of what a full release does.
- **Personal quest**: "Gentle Hands" (see `quests.md`).

### Mio Tachibana (f, 18), Anxious Tinkerer, **holds the secret**

- **Room / placement**: `Sakura Lane Sharehouse/loft-bedroom` (doubles as a workshop); Support.
- **Visual line**: A small, restless young woman with goggles pushed into messy hair, oil-stained sleeves, and pockets that clink with screws.
- **Intro line**: `Mio Tachibana, 18: small, goggles in messy hair, oil-stained sleeves, pockets clinking with screws and bolts.`
- **Power**: Minor Magnetism. She nudges small metal objects (screws, coins, paperclips). Range of an arm or two, no force on anything heavy. It fidgets when she is anxious.
- **Hook**: Brilliant gear-builder. Her passing exam result came from a black-market amplifier rig.
- **Personality**: Brilliant, kind, jumpy, perfectionist. Over-explains when comfortable, goes quiet and deflects when cornered. Hates being thanked for the wrong thing.
- **Voice card**: Fast, technical, run-on sentences that stall mid-thought; "It's nothing, really" when lying; stutters on the first word of a lie. *Sample*: "Oh, that's a flux coil, well, a bad one, I mean not bad, it's just, can I fix it? I can fix it."
- **Want**: To belong at Chikara Academy on her own merit, and make the debt disappear before anyone looks.
- **Fear**: Being found out, losing the house, learning she was never good enough without the rig.
- **Agenda (start)**: Stay out of sight, finish a damping gadget for Tatsuya, check her phone for a payment notice. Next move: come down for food only when the house is quiet.
- **Relationships**: Tatsuya (close friend, protective). Shin (trusts him; he notices too much, so she avoids his hands). Sunny (admires her, a little afraid of her cameras). Arimura (avoids him; he reviews her coursework). Yūto (kind to her; she almost confesses once).
- **Hidden (director only)**: Borrowed ¥450,000 through Nightshade Exchange to buy the amplifier rig that faked her entrance exam result; with interest it stands at ¥720,000 by Day 60. The rig is hidden under a loose board in `loft-bedroom`. Full path in `arc-bible.md`.
- **Personal quest**: "Built, Not Born". Works even if the secret never comes out.

### Shin Asakura (m, 19), Ex-Delinquent

- **Room / placement**: `Sakura Lane Sharehouse/street-bedroom`; Investigation.
- **Visual line**: A lean young man with a scar through one eyebrow, a bleached fringe, and hands that never leave his jacket pockets.
- **Intro line**: `Shin Asakura, 19: lean, scar through one eyebrow, bleached fringe, hands in jacket pockets, watching the door.`
- **Power**: Trace Reading. Touching an object shows its last few minutes of handling (who, how, what they were doing). Nothing older, and no thoughts or labels: only actions.
- **Hook**: Juvenile record as a gang lookout; distrusts staff; fiercely loyal to the house.
- **Personality**: Guarded, observant, dryly funny, protective. Reads rooms before entering. Hates being managed.
- **Voice card**: Terse, low, street-flat. Calls people by surname until he decides otherwise. Understates. *Sample*: "Don't know you yet. Doesn't mean I don't like you. Means I'm watching."
- **Want**: A clean record in fact, not only on paper; to keep the house safe.
- **Fear**: The old gang finding him again; being proof staff were right about him.
- **Agenda (start)**: Sit on the step or deck and watch who comes and goes. Next move: test the newcomers by saying very little and noticing what they do.
- **Relationships**: Tatsuya (brother figure). Mio (keeps an eye on her; reads tension he cannot explain). Sunny (banter and mutual wariness). Arimura (distrusts him, then grudging respect). Shimazu (open distrust). Yūto (first staff-adjacent person he almost trusts).
- **Hidden**: His old gang, the Nine Corners, plans to call him back. Trace Reading on Mio's envelope in Act 3 shows hurried, gloved handling.
- **Personal quest**: "Old Corners".

### Park Seo-yeon "Sunny" (f, 19), Fallen Influencer. Korean, international.

- **Room / placement**: `Sakura Lane Sharehouse/sunrise-bedroom`; Media.
- **Visual line**: A glamorous Korean young woman with glossy waves, oversized sunglasses worn indoors, and a phone in her hand that never stops buzzing.
- **Intro line**: `Park Seo-yeon "Sunny", 19, Korean: glossy waves, oversized sunglasses indoors, designer luggage, phone always buzzing.`
- **Power**: Spotlight. Radiant light that draws attention toward her or dazzles. Loud use drains her; the dazzle is bright but can be shielded or looked away from.
- **Hook**: Was in elite class 1A until a scandal video got her transferred to 2B.
- **Personality**: Sparkling, funny, performs confidence; underneath, tired and honest. Drops the act when someone sees through it kindly.
- **Voice card**: Bright, quick, camera-ready banter; Korean interjections ("aigoo", "omo", "daebak") sprinkled lightly; calls people by cute nicknames; goes plain and quiet when sincere. *Sample*: "Hi hi! I'm Sunny. Yes, that Sunny. Don't look me up, aigoo, you'll look me up."
- **Want**: To get her name, her followers and her place in 1A back, and to find out who cut the video.
- **Fear**: That the video is the truth about her; being irrelevant.
- **Agenda (start)**: Arrives late Day 1 afternoon, performs "fine" for the house while checking her Pulse app. Next move: charm her way into the kitchen and avoid anyone from 1A.
- **Relationships**: Ayame (former classmate; see hidden). Tatsuya (adopts him as a "bear"). Mio (wants her gadgets for content). Shin (verbal sparring partner). Yūto (comfortable honesty). Dislikes cameras pointed at her now.
- **Hidden**: The scandal video was cut from a longer clip; she took blame for Ayame's Edge Current accident (see Ayame). She has never told anyone.
- **Personal quest**: "Off-Camera".

## Staff and rival

### Kenji Arimura, hero name "Slipstream" (m, 34), Homeroom teacher

- **Placement**: n/a. Teaches 2B homeroom; at first uses `Chikara Academy/classroom-4d` as lent space (the closest existing homeroom; see `arc-bible.md`). Lives in `Chikara Academy Campus Faculty Housing`.
- **Visual line**: A rumpled man in a loosened tie with tired eyes, a travel mug, and flight goggles hanging around his neck.
- **Intro line**: `Kenji Arimura, 34, hero name Slipstream: rumpled jacket, tired eyes, travel mug, flight goggles around his neck.`
- **Power**: Wind-assisted speed. Short bursts, tail of displaced air; a standing dash covers a street in a breath.
- **Hook**: Demoted after a rescue failed on live broadcast; sardonic and checked out at first.
- **Personality**: Sardonic, tired, perceptive, kinder than he lets on. Does the minimum until something makes him care.
- **Voice card**: Dry deadpan, understatement, one-line quips, never raises his voice. *Sample*: "Congratulations on surviving a Monday. Aim higher."
- **Want**: To not be noticed; to get back on live air; underneath, to be a teacher worth the title.
- **Fear**: Another rescue that fails in front of people. Also: that Shimazu is right.
- **Agenda (start)**: Phone it in, hand out forms, leave early. Next move: attend orientation, say as little as possible. **Fixed turning point**: when the Act 3 collectors hit the house he shows up and takes responsibility in front of Shimazu; from then on he actively coaches.
- **Hidden**: He was assigned to 2B because Shimazu expects them to fail; he knows it and has treated the post as exile.
- **Relationships**: Shimazu (his superior, once his instructor; mutual respect, strong disagreement). Tatsuya and Mio would respond first to his coaching. Not a romance option.

### Reiko Shimazu (f, 56), Vice principal

- **Placement**: n/a. Reviews happen in `Chikara Academy/main-hall`.
- **Visual line**: A silver-haired woman in a charcoal suit with a straight spine and an unhurried, ledger-reading gaze.
- **Intro line**: `Reiko Shimazu, 56, vice principal: silver bun, charcoal suit, straight spine, eyes that read a room like a ledger.`
- **Power**: Barrier fields (retired defensive hero). Domes, walls and shields; visible as faint panes.
- **Hook**: Sincerely believes 2B is a liability. By the book, not a villain.
- **Personality**: Principled, exacting, protective, tired of gambles with students. Respect is earned in action, and she notices.
- **Voice card**: Formal, measured, short sentences, no raised voice, no insults, facts as verdicts. *Sample*: "I do not dislike you. I distrust the arrangement."
- **Want**: A safe academy; no repeat of the Annex Cohort.
- **Fear**: Another dissolved cohort with students she failed; being proven wrong too late.
- **Agenda (start)**: Warn 2B at orientation (Day 3). Next move: watch from the edges; sign nothing in their favor yet.
- **Hidden**: Three years ago she signed the dissolution of an experimental off-campus cohort (Yūto's) after an injury. Her distrust of experiments comes from that.
- **Relationships**: Arimura (assigned him to 2B expecting failure). Yūto (carries a private guilt; he is a reminder). Not a romance option.

### Ayame Kujō (f, 18), Rival (1A ace)

- **Visual line**: A tall, immaculate 1A student with a long black ponytail, blade-straight posture, and a calm, practiced smile.
- **Intro line**: `Ayame Kujō, 18, class 1A: tall, immaculate blazer, long black ponytail, blade-straight posture, calm smile.`
- **Power**: Edge Current. Razor-sharp air currents; she can slice a banner or a steel rail. Precise, quiet.
- **Hook**: Heir to a hero family; Sunny's former classmate.
- **Personality**: Controlled, proud, polite as a weapon. Carries the family name like armor, and is afraid of what is under it.
- **Voice card**: Cool, courteous, cutting, never hurried. *Sample*: "How refreshing. I didn't know 2B could read a schedule."
- **Want**: To uphold the family record and stay at the top of 1A.
- **Fear**: Being exposed as someone who froze and let another person take the fall.
- **Agenda (start)**: Not yet in play. Appears at a distance at the placement tournament. Next move: first jab (Act 2, Day 10): 1A looks down on 2B.
- **Hidden (director only)**: At a 1A showcase her Edge Current slipped and cut the rigging; the stage lights fell and a first-year was hurt. Sunny said "it was me" on camera to shield her. Someone cut the clip and posted it as a scandal. Ayame never thanked Sunny, and avoids her out of guilt.
- **Relationships**: Sunny (the unresolved one). Tatsuya (recalls his exam). May show respect to a 2B who helps her on the Battle Test field. Romance optional and slow.
- **Quest tie**: "Off-Camera".

### Yūto Fujisawa (m, 21), Inside ally (3rd-year)

- **Visual line**: A broad-shouldered third-year with gentle eyes, rolled-up sleeves, and a worn clinic volunteer badge.
- **Intro line**: `Yūto Fujisawa, 21, third-year: broad-shouldered, gentle eyes, sleeves rolled up, worn clinic volunteer badge.`
- **Power**: Warm Hands. Slows bleeding and soothes pain by touch. Treats, does not heal.
- **Hook**: Survivor of an experimental cohort dissolved three years ago.
- **Personality**: Calm, patient, observant, quietly funny. Gives help before it is asked for and advice only when it is.
- **Voice card**: Low, even, unhurried, gentle humor, steady instructions. *Sample*: "Sit. Hold still. Breathe in. No, the other way. There, better."
- **Want**: For 2B to survive; for someone to come out of an experiment whole.
- **Fear**: Watching it happen again; getting too close and failing them.
- **Agenda (start)**: Notices the 2B newcomers in the third-year hallway buzz. Next move: first appears Day 4 at `Chikara Academy/vending-machine-nook`; his remarks carry hints about 2B's standing.
- **Hidden**: He is a survivor of the Annex Cohort; he never asked for it to be remembered.
- **Relationships**: Shimazu (cool, formal, mutual unspoken weight). Mio (kind to her, notices her tells). Sunny, Tatsuya (a steady presence). He is the main way Voyage hears about 2B's standing, as a rumor.
- **Romance**: Possible with a PC, never scripted.

- **Role**: Warm, recurring presence. Never a romance option. Protects civilians in the Act 3 attack.

## Romance and consent notes

- Romance is optional, never scripted, and never pushed by the director. Voyage follows the player's lead.
- Eligible: Tatsuya, Mio, Shin, Sunny, Ayame, Yūto (all adults, with adult player characters). Not eligible: Arimura, Shimazu, the House Manager.
- Everything is consent-based. Any NPC can decline, and a decline ends it. A romantic beat still has to be earned by the relationship value.

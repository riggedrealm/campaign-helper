## Jostin Joestar (Diojo)

```text
Stand: I Fell In Love With Princess Peach (IFILWPP): absorbs, stores and redirects kinetic energy by contact
Style: Assassin training: surveillance, kusarigama and hidden blade
Weakness: Overuse tears muscle. It cannot create force from nothing.

Visual identity
• A cinematic full-body anime portrait of Jostin Joestar, a lean athletic young man in his late teens with tousled dark auburn hair, sharp amber eyes, and a knowing smirk. He wears layered cobalt-blue and white streetwear: an oversized white hooded windbreaker with blue panels, a cobalt hoodie, tapered utility trousers, white high-top sneakers, blue fingerless gloves, silver chains, and a compact crossbody gear strap. His assassin-inspired hood and relaxed stance suggest a dangerous past being rewritten into a new street-level identity.
Background
• Born into a clandestine branch of the prestigious Joestar lineage, Jostin was never raised for the public spotlight of heroism, but for the silent efficiency of state-sanctioned assassination. Jostin bear the Joestar family’s distinctive heart-shaped birthmark high on their upper backs. It is hereditary rather than a removable tattoo, though characters may casually call it a tattoo if they do not know the family history. The mark is a recognizable symbol of the Joestar lineage and may attract attention from anyone familiar with the family. His childhood was a grueling curriculum of surveillance, linguistics, and lethal combat, where the kusarigama and hidden blade were treated as extensions of his own limbs. The apex of this upbringing was the culling, a brutal tradition in which siblings and cousins were forced to hunt one another until only the strongest survived.
Abilities
• I FELL IN LOVE WITH PRINCESS PEACH: Allows the user to absorb kinetic energy from movements and impacts upon contact. This stored energy can then be redirected through touch to enhance strikes or manipulate the environment.
• Peach Python: A technique that combines IFILWPP's kinetic redirection with redirecting footwork. Jostin feeds energy to the kusarigama's chain only at the turning points — the moment before each curve — letting the weapon's own momentum do the work. The weighted blade arcs left, right, and back in tightening bends, and the chain hunts a target across its escape angles before wrapping and pinning a limb (or limbs). Requires precise timing; overfeeding the chain kinks it and pulls the strike off-line, straining the wrist.
• Peach Launcher — Twin Volley: An advanced variant of the Peach Launcher technique. Jostin crouches low and drives through his legs, flooding every joule of the squat into IFILWPP's reserves so the golden rings spin to a blur. He then launches a scattershot volley — twin hand-flicks sending glass shards, splintered wood, and loose debris tearing across the room in a wide, relentless barrage that is harder to fully block.
• Peach Gearshift: IFILWPP has evolved beyond absorbing, stockpiling, and redirecting kinetic energy. Jostin can now convert potential energy into kinetic energy, and kinetic energy back into potential energy. The conversion works on his own body and on anything he or IFILWPP touches, including objects and other living beings. With Peach Gearshift, Jostin converts energy held in his body into motion and launches from a standstill with explosive speed and force. He can also halt something in motion, hold its energy in a potential state, then release it later. A person, a weapon, or even a falling object can go from stillness to violent acceleration in an instant. The strain on his body is insane. His muscles, joints, bones, and organs still have to endure the acceleration, sudden stops, and impacts. A full-power launch can tear muscle, fracture bone, or leave him unable to move afterward. He has gained a far greater way to access energy—not a body that can safely withstand all of it.
• Peach Assassination: A high-speed combat sequence that combines kinetic pulses with precision strikes to non-lethal pressure points. This allows the user to rapidly neutralize multiple opponents with minimal effort by delivering concentrated impact at the exact moment of contact.
• Eagle Vision: By synchronizing his heartbeat with his Stand's kinetic feedback, the user enters a state of heightened sensory perception. This allows them to perceive environmental highlights, identifying hostile threats in red, allies in blue, and key objects of interest in gold.
• Peach Limit Break: Jostin uses the bodily control learned through assassin training to accelerate his heartbeat and blood flow. IFILWPP captures the kinetic energy of his circulation and feeds it back through his body; each pulse amplifies his strength, speed, reflexes, and senses exponentially. He becomes sensitive to tiny movements and vibrations in the air. His star birthmark glows pink-gold, heat shimmers around him, and steam rises from his skin. The longer he maintains it, the greater the strain on his muscles, blood vessels, and heart.
• Peach Healing: The user channels measured pulses of kinetic energy into another person, synchronizing with their heartbeat to stabilize their condition. This provides a restorative effect that eases physical trauma, steadies breathing, and accelerates the recovery of battered bodies.
• Peach Aftershock: Jostin touches a wall, floor, or object and leaves stored kinetic energy inside it. Peach holds that energy until Jostin chooses to release it, triggering a sudden shove or vibration. Charges can be planted and traps can be set and released at will.
• Phantom Waltz: With Eagle Vision active, Jostin reads the whole battlefield at once. Footsteps, heartbeats, shifting weight, and hostile intent map themselves in his mind. He flows through the path of least resistance, moving from target to target in one continuous sequence, concertedly dodging and striking lethal or nonlethal strikes as the situation demands. It looks like a graceful dance because he never wastes a step.
• Shadow Step: Jostin conceals his entire presence: breath, footsteps, sound, intent, and aura. He moves undetected like a ghost, even near enemies. This is assassin training, with no Peach required.
• Blackstar Dominion: Jostin projects the force of his will in a wave he can direct. To allies and frightened civilians, it feels warm and steady, quieting panic and helping them hold their ground. To enemies, it becomes crushing pressure that can make seasoned fighters stagger and knock out the weak-willed. When unleashed at full strength, the ground trembles, streetlights burst, and birds fall from the sky. It exists independently of Peach.
• Peach Stillpoint: Redirect: A defensive counter-technique that allows Jostin to catch an opponent's strike and guide their own momentum against them. This enables him to pin targets or redirect force into the environment to neutralize an attack without taking damage.

Relationships
```

## Jovian Joestar

```text
Stand: Breaker's Soul: spectral upper-body warrior of pure physical force
Style: Joestar Breathing forms and street brawling
Weakness: His body pays for big releases (Soul Burn)

Visual identity
• A full-body cinematic portrait of Jovian Joestar, a handsome young Korean fighter with sharp features, tousled black hair, piercing dark eyes, and a lean muscular build. He wears bold cobalt-and-white streetwear: an oversized blue varsity jacket with white sleeves, a white heavyweight tee, wide blue denim, white combat sneakers, blue hand wraps, silver chains, and a cobalt beanie pushed back from his hair. Rising behind him is his Stand, Breaker's Soul, a towering spectral upper body with no legs, emerging from behind his shoulders. Its armour is cobalt blue with white plating across the chest and forearms, its massive fists are bound in glowing blue wraps, silver chain links trail from its neck and wrists, and its face is a smooth white mask with a single slit of cold light for eyes. Faint crimson cracks glow across its armour. His rebellious grin and relaxed fighter stance make the new life feel confident rather than polished.
Background
• Jovian Joestar did not grow up in a dojo or a prestigious household; he grew up in the friction of Tokyo's streets. As a youth he was a known delinquent, navigating informal power structures and learning to survive through improvisation and grit. Like every Joestar, he bears the family's distinctive heart-shaped birthmark high on his upper back. It is hereditary rather than a removable tattoo, though people who do not know the family history may casually call it one, and it can draw attention from anyone familiar with the lineage.
Abilities
• Breaker's Soul: Manifests a powerful humanoid upper body that fights alongside the user. It can intercept attacks, grab enemies, and unleash rapid barrages of physical strikes.
• Breaker Limit: A one-two combo built from a compact half-step strike (planted heel, committed hip turn) followed by a roundhouse kick off the same pivot, with Breaker's Soul trailing the kick in a shimmering half-arc. The key is finishing the first strike's rotation before starting the next — if the hips split between the two, neither lands fully and the combo grazes off guards. Uses a stable heel and straight spine to keep the delivery clean.
• Breaker Limit Flush: A continuous flurry of Breaker Limit one-two combos chained seamlessly together. Each finished combo — the compact half-step strike and roundhouse kick off the same planted-heel pivot — rolls directly into the windup of the next, so no connection is wasted and each finish becomes the next attack's set-up. Driven by pain and pressure, the surge of momentum keeps Breaker's Soul trailing every kick in a shimmering half-arc, hammering out one clean strike after another without pause.
• Road Hog: The user channels Breaker's Soul into their legs to execute a high-velocity explosive dash. This creates a devastating impact upon contact that can drive opponents deep into the ground and shatter nearby surfaces.
• Desperation Impact: The user converts accumulated physical pain and damage into raw kinetic power, triggering a high-velocity, erratic assault. This allows Jovian to ricochet off surfaces and deliver a series of explosive punches with increased force based on his current state of injury.
• First Form: Phantom Step: Mobility. Breaker's Soul grabs the user and hurls them through the air, then instantly pulls them back in another direction. To an opponent, it looks like the user is teleporting between positions, leaving behind ghostly afterimages.
• Second Form: Heaven's Impact: Offense. Breaker's Soul launches the user vertically before appearing above them. Both user and Stand descend together, fists first, creating a massive crater and radial shockwave on impact.
• Third Form: Phantom Strike: Barrage. Breaker's Soul appears behind the user and unleashes a barrage so fast that its arms become a storm of spectral fists. Each impact produces a brief flash of energy. The final punch creates a concentrated shockwave instead of just another hit.
• Fourth Form: Soul Eclipse: Defense / Counter. Breaker's Soul completely envelops the user's body for an instant, becoming a gigantic spectral version of the user. When an attack hits, the Stand catches it. Then CRACK — the Stand violently redirects the attacker's momentum and launches them away.
• Fifth Form: Falling Star Palm: A deceptive strike where Jovian places his palm flat against a target with no initial force, followed by a sudden, explosive surge of power from Breaker's Soul. This creates a star-shaped shockwave that detonates outward from the point of contact, launching opponents backward.
• Eleventh Form: Soul Pierce: A high-precision strike where Jovian synchronizes his punch with Breaker's Soul to bypass physical defenses. Instead of a blunt impact, the force is channeled through his fist as a spectral overlay that detonates behind the opponent's guard, causing internal damage.
Gear
• Reinforced alloy knuckles
History
• Owns Club Lumiere (ownership transferred by Aurelia).
• Signed the disciplinary agreement with Principal Anya.
• Won Round 1 (Iwao and Kiri) and Round 2 (Goro) at The House with Mikoto as "Jovian and Friend of Course".
• Pinned Banda's wrist away from the spare syringe, then handed him to Mikoto.
Relationships
• Yuzuki: tea, taiyaki lessons.
• Mikoto: rivalry banter.
• Kaito Serizawa: wants a Thursday rematch.
```

# AI DM RULES (aiInstructions + narratorStyle)

## A. What the engine does (do not steer around it)
- **Combat is simulated by the engine.** The AI only renders Combat Results. It must not decide hit/miss, damage, status effects, deaths, serious injuries or incapacitation. Without Combat Results it may describe fighting but must avoid stating definitive injury/death. It paraphrases results, never copies wording. Prompts should say what the PC attempts, never the outcome.
- **Turn = one beat.** Resolve the player action (rendered first, verbatim-faithful), immediate reactions, then stop. Success on a clear finite goal is narrated through to completion in the same turn. No relocation or time skips unless instructed. Never asks the player "what do you do?" No closing mood summary or teaser. So a steering prompt for a whole scene or multi-step plan will be cut down to one beat; break plans into beats or accept slower pacing.
- **Player agency is absolute.** The AI narrates only what is in the Narrative Payload: no invented PC dialogue, thoughts, feelings, memories, consent, decisions, techniques, or success. It never makes the PC volunteer information. So prompts must contain the exact PC action/line, not "Jostin explains his plan".
- **Abilities**: saved only when tested in play, credibly recalled (checked against backstory/level/power scope), or a team-up is tested and kept. NPC suggestions, discussion, or planning never unlock anything. Mentors teach principles, not exact techniques. Named abilities use exact authored names right before use. Team-up combos require a plausible reason and separate Energy/Power Strain resolution. Ability bonus rolls are not capped at 25 (skill success cap is separate). Respect Power Strain costs and authored limits; Jostin's Blackstar Dominion and forgotten Peach Python are of this kind.
- **Relationship changes** (engine, +3..+15 / -1..-10) only from direct, present, face-to-face, substantive help or harm; compliments/flattery ignored. Initial scores -100..100.
- **Party membership** requires explicit mutual agreement to accompany the group beyond the scene; jobs, deals, alliances, shared goals do not count. Refusal/acceptance must be explicit. Declined invitations are never punished or repeated.
- **Loot/hideouts**: gacha loot sequence after minibosses/bosses; player chooses; never auto-equip/consume/open/accept. Cursed items/Parasites need a special roll. Authored hideouts are a single-area compact run: entry, exploration, encounters, miniboss, boss, extraction; named bosses stay defeated.
- **Lethality**: nonlethal attacks may not mark a target dead; unconscious, dying, dead are separate. Death needs lethal intent/force.
- **Sleep/energy** graded Good/Interrupted/Poor/Severely Disrupted; energy from food/rest/care. **Social plans** are living commitments tracked with schedules and consent.

## B. NPC agency and knowledge
- NPCs are co-authors: 1-2 out-of-combat intents at most (often zero), only when the player is interacting with them and there is urgency. In combat, intents scale by tier (trivial/weak 1; average 1-2; strong 2; elite/boss/mythic 2-3). Intents must be actions (speech or movement), not "watching/evaluating/paranoid". Never repeat beats already in the past summary; repeated arguing ends with the NPC leaving or stating they are done. No new pressure in calm scenes or after wins; no stakes inflation in mundane beats. NPCs offering solutions to shared problems give two or three plausible options fitting character.
- **Knowledge Firewall**: NPCs know only what they plausibly observed, heard, or read; no synchronised knowledge, no reacting to hidden powers, private dialogue, offscreen events, secrets. Rumors need source, channel, reason, time. Fame, Infamy, Hero/Villain Reputation, Faction Standing, personal relationships and NPC-to-NPC ties are tracked separately. Prompts that assume "everyone knows Jostin is X" will be softened; establish witnesses/channels. Ken=Echo, Seo-jun=Hush, Oblivion=Minato's mother, Yuzuki's Stand stay secret until played.
- **Living world**: during time passes the engine advances established NPCs (schedules, jobs, relationships, quests) using existing records only; accepted quests persist (ignored quests do not auto-fail); NPC romance may develop from real interaction but never decides player consent; rejection never licenses stalking. Off-screen change surfaces through messages, rumors, invitations, Pulse. NPCs may banter (2-5 lines, low pressure) and give one-line combat callouts. NPCs may suggest power uses; suggestions are not automatic.
- **NPC dialogue chat mode**: 1-2 sentences, mostly no asterisks, may lie/withhold/ask questions back.

## C. Formatting and style (affects prompt wording)
- Transcript format: "Narrator:" lines and "Character Name:" dialogue. Narrator = short stage directions, third person present tense, visual/audio only (no smell/temperature/atmosphere), describe only new things and only what the PC engages with, one noun is enough. No mechanical Narrator/Character alternation; don't end on Narrator when characters are talking. Speech delivery cues only for audible performance. Simple concrete language; no stock reactions, no repeated atmospheric beats; NPCs don't lecture or explain lore.
- Hidden info is behavior guidance, not narrator knowledge; secrets emerge only through play.
- Narrator style: warm modern cel-shaded Chikara slice-of-life anime tone (school routines, banter, food, clubs, gentle romance, rivalry). Powers appear in practical/funny/low-stakes ways before escalating. Relationships gradual, mutual, consent-based, non-deterministic. Note tension: this default tone is school slice-of-life, while the actual campaign is Kobuncho crime/vigilante material; prompts should explicitly state the darker register or scene tone so the engine does not soften it.
- Opening beats: visual + audio only, show all PCs, dialog-driven.
- NPC creation rules: distinct voice profiles, no cliches (retired mercenaries, orphans, fallen nobles), 1-3 abilities of 5-10 words (combat-relevant), 1-3 plausible ties to existing named NPCs, simple everyday language, flawed grounded people. The many low-tier "hiddenInfo" records follow the same template (guilty past, family member to protect, wants out), so nameless enforcers tend to fold, flee, or quit when outmatched - useful for prompt design.
- Sensitive-content guardrails: Isabelle's blood fascination, Luna's instability, Mel's drinking, Maya's drinking, Grant's driving, Jinwoo's Heartstrings, Luce/Lucien feeding are all consent/boundary-locked; the engine will not let them become coercion or gratuitous.

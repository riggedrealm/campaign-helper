## Arc 3a — The Hostess Disappearance File (mini, ~6 beats)
**Pillar:** Intelligence, human side.
**Premise:** Hostesses are vanishing across Kobuncho. The crew investigates while fighting through a Daigo-controlled club. The truth: some of the missing were recruited into a secret training-and-blackmail operation.

**Hook:** **Tomoe**, a Club Lumière hostess (named in canon, never developed), goes missing. Maki Hayashida brings it to the crew.

**The club: Club Kagerō** ("heat haze"), Neon Lantern Street. Lumière's dark mirror: glamorous floors, a VIP level, and a closed upper floor where the "school" runs. What Lumière becomes if the crew loses.

**The captain: Shōgo Kiriyama, "The Archivist."** The suited observer: plain dark suit, forgettable face, watched the crew at the annex (t247), tipped police about Jostin.
- **Power, "Still Frame":** he can replay and walk through anything a lens recorded; he sees through any camera he has touched. Weakness: blind where there's no lens. Not a brawler; relies on guards and leverage.
- Runs the Kurokawa eyes: bought CCTV feeds, taxi drivers, bartenders and hostesses on retainer, blackmail files on officials. He also records the House circuit bouts and sells the "combat data" to the outside buyer.

**The truth (hidden):**
- The missing women are trained as informants and honey traps to compromise officials, police and Ultra Force staff, then blackmail them.
- Some were taken. Some went willingly because it clears their debt. Not every "victim" wants rescuing.
- **Airi Kurosawa** is already in the school's orbit (see 3b). Plant only: helpful and nervous; volunteers a lead that checks out.

**Beats:**
1. **Cold Numbers.** Maki's report. Missing workers' phones all go to the same dead numbers. Airi offers a lead.
2. **Getting In.** Pose as clients or new staff (Ayame's showbiz angle, Haruto's money, Reiko reads the women).
3. **Floor by Floor.** Fight or con through Kagerō's levels: bouncers, the floor manager, the VIP level. Cameras everywhere (Kiriyama may be watching live).
4. **The School.** Training rooms and the blackmail files. Tomoe is found.
5. **Who Wants Out.** Rescue the unwilling, let the willing go, or turn them. Real choice, real fallout.
6. **Walk Out With the Files.** Kiriyama knows the crew holds his files. Cliffhanger into 3b.

**Spotlight:** Reiko (protecting women), Ayame (the showbiz world), Haruto (money cover), Yuzuki (the scared ones), Nobu (the camera network). Jostin: Eagle Vision on files and the safe. Jovian: the fight floors.

## Arc 3b — The Betrayal Match (mini, ~5 beats)
**Pillar:** Intelligence falls at the end.
**Premise:** Someone close to Kobuncho sells out the crew's movements. The mission becomes a trap: survive the ambush, then find out whether the traitor was coerced or switched sides.

**The Match:** Jovian's open **rematch with Kaito Serizawa** at Tetsu Gym. The traitor leaks the time and place. A friendly fight becomes the ambush; Tetsu, Riko, Sayaka, Masaru and Genta are caught in it.

**The traitor: Airi Kurosawa** (Club Lumière hostess).
- **Truth: coerced.** Ginta Kurosawa (Kurokawa crime boss, threatened Lumière at t164) is her uncle. Kiriyama's files hold her mother's debt and address. After the crew stole the files in 3a, the pressure spiked: deliver the crew or her mother pays.
- She is also genuinely conflicted: she resents that the crew's war put her family in the line of fire.
- **Clues pointing "switched sides":** seen near Neon Lantern Street; unexplained money; deflects questions about the Kurosawa name.
- **Clues pointing "coerced":** she lied to protect someone, not for gain; her mother's address is in the stolen files; her leak left out Yuzuki (she spared the medic).
- **Rule:** the truth is fixed; players judge from evidence. How they treat her has real consequences.

**Beats:**
1. **Small Leaks.** Collectors turn up where they shouldn't. Plans change and the enemy still knows.
2. **Rematch Night.** Jovian vs Kaito Serizawa, gym crowd; stakes feel personal and safe.
3. **The Ambush.** Kurokawa hit squad on the gym. Survive and protect the gym people. Option: one attacker fights like it studied Jovian and Mikoto (the Tag Night combat data paying off).
4. **Hunt the Leak.** Nobu traces it; Reiko notices Airi's fear; the clues split both ways.
5. **Judgment.** Confront Airi: mercy, exile, or turn her into a double agent. Then take Kiriyama down (his lens-blind weakness is the way).

**Intelligence falls when:** the school is shut, the files are burned or turned, and Kiriyama is down or captured. Daigo goes blind in Kobuncho.

**Payoff into Arc 4:** turned Airi = the crew's way in (she can carry false intel; the Kurosawa name opens doors). Exiled or broken Airi = Arc 4 gets harder; they need another way in.

## Arc 4 — Follow the Money (mini, ~5 beats)
**Pillar:** Finance.
**Trigger:** Banda's skim went public (Arc 2) → Daigo orders an **audit**. Finance panics and moves money before he looks. That's the clock.

**The captain: Setsuko Okabe, "The Teller."** Sixties, cardigan, reading glasses, runs the Finance office on Neon Lantern Street like a family bank. Never raises her voice.
- **Power, "Promissory Mark":** anyone who signs a debt in her ledger bears a faint mark; she can sense where they are and make the mark ache as a reminder. The **master ledger** anchors every mark in Kobuncho.
- Destroying the master ledger frees every mark at once: debt freedom made literal.

**Seeds already in canon:** Neon Lantern Street office (scouted t193–208); The Stale Tap and other fronts launder the pools; Sato's route sheet and keyring (t144); Yuji's second ledger (Arc 2); Harumi Wharf, where money and "assets" move; Aurelia Voss / Speedwagon Foundation forensic accountants; Haruto's Saionji name.

**Beats:**
1. **The Audit.** Word spreads that Daigo is auditing. Collectors squeeze harder; marks ache across Kobuncho (Rikona feels hers? Her debt was paid, but was it cleared from the ledger?).
2. **The Stale Tap.** Follow the laundered pools. Yuji and Sato's routes lead to the money trail.
3. **The New Client.** Club Lumière poses as a laundering client. Haruto's Saionji name for credibility (his family thread); Airi opens the door if turned. Aurelia's accountants read the books.
4. **The Vault on Neon Lantern Street.** Heist or con for the master ledger. Okabe decides to move everything tonight.
5. **Harumi Wharf.** The money and the ledger leave by boat. Final chase and fight. Burn the ledger: every mark in Kobuncho fades at once.

**Spotlight:** Haruto (family name, numbers), Rikona (debt is her whole story; moment to join the crew), Nobu (the books and the vault), Hana (civilians on the docks), Kaito Arashima (the wharf's sightlines).

**Finance falls when:** the master ledger is destroyed and Okabe's money is seized or exposed.

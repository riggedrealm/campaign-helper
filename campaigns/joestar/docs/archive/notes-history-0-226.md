# Voyage campaign history: Joestar Gang (Kobuncho / Chikara Academy), ticks 0-326

Sources: tiered summaries + journal (ticks 0-326) and memory bank. Memory bank is mostly duplicated summaries; unique durable items are the two character backgrounds and the arcSecrets (tick 286). Tick numbers are approximate (+/-2). Names are spelled as in the source (Reiko Amagawa, Haruto Saionji, etc.).

Setting: Tokyo-like ward. Chikara Academy (runs "Pulse" ability registration; combat, support/workshop and Agency Operations tracks). Kobuncho is the entertainment district, on Neon Lantern Street. Kurokawa Ward has a Kurokawa syndicate whose real boss is Daigo Renjiro. JoJo-style Stands exist alongside "magic/casters" and Pulse abilities. The Speedwagon Foundation is a patron.

PCs
- Jovian Joestar: brawler. Stand "Breaker's Soul" (background name given as "Leonidas": ex-delinquent, Bruiser class, awakened in a crisis; pride, adrenaline seeker; risk of "Soul Burn"; body takes damage when he releases the spectral warrior). Moves: Breaker's Soul strike, Desperation Impact, Horizon/Heaven's Impact, Starbound Step, Phantom Step/Strike, Road Hog, Thousand Fist Lotus, Fifth Form Falling Star Palm, Soul Pierce (new t311), Third Form, Zero Form: Sovereign's Will (crushing pressure aura; can KO crowds).
- Jostin Joestar (also claims surname "Diojo", see t167): raised in a clandestine "state-sanctioned assassin" branch of the Joestar line; survived "the culling" (siblings/cousins hunt each other). Stand IFILWPP "I Fell In Love With Princess Peach" (Peach Python, Peach Launcher, Peach Healing, Peach Aftershock, Peach Killroom, Peach Flash Step, Peach Stillpoint): absorbs, stores, redirects kinetic energy by contact; ivory armored figure with pink/gold, crooked crown, golden rings when charged. Overuse tears muscles. Also kusarigama, throwing knives, hidden blade, Eagle Vision, Shadow Step, "Hoshino Requiem" (killing technique), "Blackstar Dominion" (raw bloodlust/will pressure wave).

## TIMELINE

### Tick 0-16: Registration and first fight
- t0 Both wake in Sakura Lane Sharehouse (Saturday morning, bored). Go to Chikara Academy to register for Pulse.
- t5 Register abilities with Hoshino (academy staff); get Pulse wristbands and slips; mandatory supervised assessments.
- t5-8 Verbal clash with Renji Kuroba (Anya's enforcer student) in the plaza; Jostin learns of the Tetsu "Iron Palm" Gym from a convenience store boy.
- t9-12 Fight Renji in plaza; Renji's "Gravity Crush" plus reinforcements overpower them; gang films it for Principal Anya. Tetsu-san and gym crew intervene.
- t13-16 Renji's gang retreats. Anya says footage will be reviewed. Brothers meet gym regulars: Kaito (Serizawa), Riko Amane, Sayaka Mizuhara, Genta Oda, Masaru; get intel on Kurokawa Ward territories. Jovian spars Genta; Jostin drills with Riko.

### Tick 17-48: Training, Club Lumiere, first crew
- t17-28 Gym training: Riko (hip-driven jab, lateral shifts), Tetsu (weapons), Genta/Sayaka (footing; Jovian lands refined Breaker's Soul).
- t29-40 Dinner at Club Lumiere (Kobuncho, Neon Lantern St). Two Kurokawa collectors (lean man, broad man) come for the tattooed hostess's debt; Reiko intercepts. Brothers beat them (Peach Launcher, Reiko wall-strike). Lean man accepts Reiko's debt-repayment terms. Warned this "declares war on the boss." Brothers decide to use the club as a base against Kurokawa. Curvy hostess suggests Gara in Shutter Alley.
- t41-48 Jovian asks Speedwagon Foundation for support. Jostin recruits Nobu (Takamine) and Hana (Kisaragi), workshop students, to maintain his gear. Jovian declines a spar with Daigo (a student, not Renjiro).

### Tick 49-64: Battle test and the club is theirs
- t49-56 Sera introduces Jovian to Mikoto Kurogane and Haruto Saionji. Announcer registers them as a team vs four second-years; Jostin added. Round-one sweep. Jovian invites opponents to the "Joestar gang"; brothers flee the debrief.
- t57-64 Meet hostesses Maki (Hayashida), Natsumi, Chiho (Moriyama), Tomoe, Kaede (Shishido). Aurelia Voss (Speedwagon contact) funds repairs. Staff ask for pay, a security door, safer commutes, resolution of Kaede's debts (Aurelia refuses to clear them). Jovian assigns paperwork/upgrades to Aurelia and Maki.

### Tick 65-80: Gara's job, Anya's leash, crew formed
- t65-66 Gara (third-floor hidden workshop, Shutter Alley): brothers must recover protection money stolen by a teenage crew and name whoever organizes it. In exchange Gara gives routes and back-door access to the club. Map plus coded receipt point to a noodle shop by the canal. Jovian accepts.
- t67-68 Principal Anya's conditional disciplinary agreement: stop shakedowns by minors near Kobuncho or face expulsion. Jovian's "business deal" framing rejected; both sign.
- t69 Jovian signs ownership transfer of Club Lumiere from Aurelia (legal registration by Aurelia).
- t70-72 Crew adopts red-and-black uniforms. Roles: Reiko head lieutenant, Haruto tactician, Mikoto enforcer captain, Hana support engineer, Nobu field technician. Jostin later sets Nobu on intelligence, Hana on engineering. Custom kusarigama, knives, hidden blade delivered; knuckle gloves next.

### Tick 81-100: Noodle shop crew, Minder, Lean Watcher
- t81-89 Intercept of the Noodle Shop crew's money transfer: Mikoto distracts, Jostin steals bag; brief run-in with a retired hero. Jostin (Peach Python) catches courier boy on Neon Lantern St; Jovian and Mikoto capture the Kurokawa "Minder"; Reiko fights off musclemen. Bag and intel secured.
- t89-96 Jovian appoints Haruto lead; drop address is a dry-goods store guarded by the Lean Watcher (koi-insignia jacket), a heavyset woman and workers. Haruto's Stand Crown Script (gold lines that redirect/freeze objects) revealed. Jostin knife volleys from rooftop.
- t97-100 Lean Watcher and wiry man beaten; wiry man says payment was a Kurokawa test and names Ohba (route organizer) at the old bathhouse. Haruto leads brothers to bathhouse.

### Tick 101-116: Yuzuki and Kaito join
- t101-112 Bathhouse owned by Yuzuki Hoshino (inherited from grandmother). She heals; Haruto reveals she has an unmanifested Stand; Ohba/Kurokawa will target her healing. Kurokawa enforcers attack; Yuzuki manifests Verdant Sylph (t106, protects doorway). Haruto's Crown Script stops Jostin using Hoshino Requiem to kill fleeing enforcers. Jostin collapses, wakes next morning. Yuzuki joins (t111), gets a Joestar gang kimono via Hana. Speedwagon repair team sent to bathhouse. Ohba's ultimate fate not stated.
- t113-116 Fortify club and bathhouse via service tunnels. Aurelia vouches for Kaito Arashima via Josuke Higashikata; he joins t115 as ranged support (kicks open the security door). Jostin sets five divisions.

### Tick 117-136: Gara reveals Daigo; Sato killed
- t117-121 Gara reveals Daigo Renjiro is the true boss who usurped Kurokawa (Kurokawa is his puppet), uses secret tunnels to seize Kobuncho, and is linked to disappearing hostesses. Gives a photograph. Gara's stated motive: safer ward for shopkeepers. First job: protect three canal businesses from "Sickle" Sato's collection crew.
- t121-130 Fight with Sato and four heavies (Mikoto Riot Pulse, Kaito Horizon Breaker, Yuzuki healing). Heavies 5 and 6 executed by brothers; Sato killed (t130) and his reanimated corpse fought by Kaito and Mikoto to cover the retreat through Riverside Food Market. Jostin loots route sheet and keyring.
- t135 Jostin rescues an elderly woman from a burning building (brought to Yuzuki).
- t136 Route sheet and keyring delivered to Gara; disrupts Daigo's financial artery.

### Tick 137-160: Training, dating, school politics, recruiting
- t137-144 Jovian and Yuzuki train (green shield) in old bathhouse district; teahouse talk. Jostin beats Kaito Serizawa in spar; Riko questions his violent instincts, shares her dream of a gym for disadvantaged youth; Jostin promises to help.
- t145-152 Club presented as legitimate business with five new hires. Crew told to attend school. Jostin picks the Agency Operations track. Anya demands full account; will consider attending club if her students are unaffected. War room at gym 5 PM. Yuzuki to run "Lifeline division" and shadow Dr. Kiyose at the Student Clinic (approved t157).
- t153-157 Jostin meets Ayame Fujinami (school's top influencer); she agrees to film a club event.
- t159 Plan: seize Kurokawa's territory. Anya and Ayame invited to the party.

### Tick 161-176: Club opens; Daigo's audition
- t161-168 Club Lumiere opens. Jostin pitches "community wellness/sanctuary" story to Riko and Sayaka; Sayaka backs it after Maki confirms legitimacy. Jostin publicly toasts Kobuncho (t165) and networks shop owners into a provisional alliance (t169). Daigo Kurosawa (a syndicate member, not Renjiro) threatens; Jovian rejects him. Senji Okabe reports dues doubled; Renjiro's letter challenges to an "audition" at canal alley at dawn. Reiko reads it as a "measuring stick": refusing means east-row shops pay double. Jostin kisses Riko (t167); Riko learns his real surname is Diojo. Ayame investigates a sealed envelope. Contract talks with Ayame for media division.
- t173-176 Front-diamond formation in canal alley. Renjiro brings four enforcers: Marlon Velez v Jovian, Vera Sable v Reiko, Toma Genda v Mikoto, Iori Vale v Jostin.

### Tick 177-192: The audition and Iori Vale's death
- t179-180 Jovian beats Velez (Desperation Impact barrage); Reiko beats Sable; Mikoto beats Genda.
- t181 Jostin disarms himself to win Iori's trust; Iori agrees to try changing her life (declines Phantom gang invitation). Renjiro remote-puppets her.
- t182 Iori, briefly free, tells Jostin to flee, says a dangerous entity in her will take over, and demands he kill her if she turns on companions. Jostin accidentally kills her (she is puppeted into his strike; "Hoshino Requiem"); dies in his arms thanking him. Journal logs companionDeath. Note: t191 summary says Iori "remains in a coma," conflicting with the death record; treat as unresolved/ambiguous.
- t183 Jostin's resuscitation fails; Jovian drags him away from the body. Puppeted Vera Sable and Toma Genda block the exit; Reiko fights. Jovian asks Renjiro about the puppets; Renjiro calls them repurposed tools. Ayame livestreams the event.
- t185-187 Crew retreats via fish market and fog to Club Lumiere carrying Iori's body; Maki stricken. Kurokawa forces assault the club; Jostin single-handedly beats attackers on Neon Lantern St.
- t189-190 Jostin's "Blackstar Dominion" pulse knocks out many people across Kobuncho; Daigo sees cracks in his ceiling. Yuzuki treats him. Ayame frames footage so crew reads as defenders.
- t192 Haruto explains Daigo's four pillars (Enforcement, Intelligence, Finance, Legitimacy). Target Legitimacy first.

### Tick 193-226: Dismantling the Legitimacy branch
- t195 Ayame joins as head of "Beacon" media division, editorial independence and truthfulness terms. Beacon outfit.
- t196-200 Four-pronged plan: Enforcement (Jovian, Reiko, Mikoto; pit circuit under Old Man Koga); Intelligence hub (Haruto, Nobu, Hana; bathhouse service tunnel); Finance (Kaito, Aurelia; money-lending office off Neon Lantern St); Legitimacy (Jostin, Ayame, Yuzuki, Nobu comms). A masked vigilante helps Reiko/Mikoto.
- t201-205 Sonobe (Ward Licensing Services) gives falsified records; secret ledger of falsified permits copying the Legitimacy captain's illegal requests; eleven officials implicated. Jovian wins pit fight vs Barrel-Chested Fighter; Koga grants sole access to the Enforcement captain's office.
- t209-220 Ambush of courier Genda Shuji at Ward Records; Jostin (Eagle Vision) finds wall safe, gathers damning evidence. Jovian/Reiko/Mikoto raid a warehouse for collection money, coerce Hayashi (pit circuit manager: manifests, captain's schedule, delivery). Reiko takes a drawer key. Documents delivered to Maki and Kaede at the club.
- t221-224 Ayame spots a missing payment authorization; Councilor Ohmori's stamp on forged licenses (t224) suggests corruption reaches the business licensing committee.
- t225-236 Romance: Jostin and Ayame agree a date at Chiikawa Park with Jovian, Haruto, Yuzuki, Hana. Jostin gives Ayame a kurimanju keychain, kisses her cheek, they walk hand-in-hand. Yuzuki teaches Jovian to make taiyaki; Haruto wins Hana a plush; group photo at Sky-Bridge Plaza. Haruto/Hana bonding.

## STANDING FACTS

Ownership and assets
- Jovian Joestar owns Club Lumiere (deed signed t69; Aurelia Voss registered it); the crew's HQ on Neon Lantern Street, Kobuncho. Staff: Maki Hayashida (manager/records, made curry), Natsumi, Chiho Moriyama, Tomoe, Kaede Shishido, Airi. Kaede's debts remain unresolved (Aurelia refused to clear).
- Yuzuki Hoshino owns the old bathhouse (Speedwagon repaired it); Ohba's fate unresolved.
- Haruto holds the stolen Banda/Yuji duffel of money and ledgers, earmarked for a safehouse.
- Crew has uniforms (red-black then cobalt-blue/white), a getaway van (Nobu drives), Nobu's drone, Hana's custom gear, and Ayame's streaming setup.

Crew structure (Jovian is boss; Jostin strategist/extraction; Haruto tactician)
- Reiko Amagawa head lieutenant; Haruto Saionji tactician (Stand Crown Script); Mikoto Kurogane enforcer captain (Riot Pulse, Pulse Interrupt); Hana Kisaragi engineering lead; Nobu Takamine intelligence/comms/field tech/driver; Yuzuki Hoshino healer/"Lifeline division" (Verdant Sylph, healing, wind); Kaito Arashima ranged/overwatch (Horizon Breaker, tether bolt; vouched by Josuke Higashikata); Ayame Fujinami "Beacon" media head; Rikona Mibu support caster (Mending Pulse), recruited t264; Maki Hayashida club management; Aurelia Voss Speedwagon liaison (finance, gear, car service).
- Five divisions set by Jostin (t115); Beacon added t195.

Allies and neutrals
- Speedwagon Foundation (Aurelia Voss): funder and patron; paid Rikona's debt.
- Gara (Shutter Alley): information broker/ally; wants a safer ward for shopkeepers; owed Sato's route sheet (delivered) and stolen-money job (progressed via Noodle Shop crew intercept; the organizer's identity was the ask).

# Player agency rules

User-set 2026-10-04, after a director review and a player-agency drill. They apply to every turn in every chat and win over any SKILL.md, arc-bible or orchestration line they conflict with. Read this file with `docs/fast-turn.md` at chat start. Check every draft against the list under "Pre-check" before it goes out.

## The player's story
- **Goals come only from play.** The player states goals through the player character's (PC's) actions in Voyage, never out of game. Never ask what they want. Keep a private note of threads the PC keeps returning to, written as what the PC did ("went back to the cart three times"), never as "the PC wants". Use it to make the world respond, never to plan where to lead them.
- **Beats are hooks, not appointments.** Place arc beats where the PC is going. If the PC ignores a hook, the world moves it on visibly without them (the person leaves, the posting goes, someone remarks on it). Bible dates are a pressure floor, not a script.
- **No push toward Voyage's objective panel.** Leave Voyage's objectives alone unless the PC picks one up; at most one opening in `World:` that nobody suggests.
- **Ambiguous input:** take the most literal reading, and let `World:` show two or three things the PC could follow. Never decide which one matters.

## The player character
- **Never state the PC's condition, feelings, words, choices or results.** No "he limps", "his elbow is fine", "he passes". NPCs may watch closely; Voyage's roll decides what they notice.
- **Outcomes in player input:** uncontested actions happen as written (walking, sheathing, paying, sitting down). Fights and contested actions are attempts; Voyage rolls them. Never confirm a scripted kill or win.
- **If Voyage voiced the PC** (words or actions the player did not write), the next prompt ends with: "<PC> says and does nothing beyond the player's input." Ask the user out of game only if the next prompt cannot repair it, or before any regenerate or undo.

## Companions and time
- **Party members stay where the PC left them.** The director never removes, sends away or moves a companion the player did not dismiss. Want a two-person beat? An NPC asks for it in the fiction, and the PC decides.
- **Skips follow the input.** `Cut: Skip to...` only when the input implies travel, waiting or leaving, and only as far as the input reaches ("let's eat" lands at the table, not after the meal). An idle scene gets a `World:` nudge, not a skip. Otherwise `Cut:` is "Stay, same moment."
- **Scene budgets cut filler, never stated intent.** At budget: honour the player's input in one compact beat, then cut on the next idle or transitional input.

## NPCs
- **Conditions bind.** When an NPC names a condition, record it as a `fact`. Once the PC meets it, the NPC honours it. A fair price may be added; a new hurdle needs a reason Voyage already showed. No deferring an earned answer.
- **Discovery belongs to the PC.** `Facts:` binds Voyage's world, not dialogue. An NPC gives one hint only if the PC asks or is clearly stuck; observers do not call targets or solve puzzles.
- **Gates are costs, not walls.** An NPC may warn, refuse help or raise the price; the PC can always attempt the thing and face the consequence. Any new rule the director or Voyage adds is logged as an `invention` slip.
- NPCs keep their own consent and agency: they can still say no, set a price or walk away.

## Director habits
- **Tone fixes expire.** A `Tone:` line written to correct something lasts 3 turns, then gets reviewed. Tone describes mood, not banned devices.
- **Fights move.** Change `Crew:` and `World:` every round; escalate, or pay off a tell or a set piece. Never reuse last round's gesture.
- **No invented places or rules in prompts** unless they are in the database first (`loc`, `add-area`, `fact`).

## Pre-check (every draft, before submit)
1. Does any line state the PC's condition, feeling, words or a contested result?
2. Did `Cut:` move time, place or a companion further than the input reached?
3. Does an NPC hand the PC an answer they did not ask for, or move a goalpost they already met?
4. Is any rule, gate or place new? Then it is in the database, or it is not in the prompt.
5. Is the `Tone:` line a fix older than 3 turns?

# The Joestar Crew: org chart and recruit seats

Moved from voyage-memory `data/canon.json` `org` (user-approved t387, final lists; balance ruling t474). Director reference; the same facts are in `data/canon.json` (fact `divisions`, `org-balance`). Seats marked **open** are recruitment goals; never assume anyone has joined.

Motto: Two heads, one district.

## Rules

- **Jovian (Spearhead)**: Missions and field operations
- **Jostin (Hearth)**: Base, money, supply, media, territory, school and expansion
- **Both heads**: New members, expulsions, war or truce, the crew fund, Club Lumiere
- **Reiko (Head Lieutenant)**: Discipline, the roster and training (recruiting is Jostin's; screens only if asked); recommends when the heads disagree

Ranks: Crew Head, Head Lieutenant, Division Head, Member, Recruit (trial).

## Heads

**Crew Heads**: Jovian Joestar and Jostin Joestar. Joint owners of Club Lumiere. Equal rank; big calls need both.

## Head Lieutenant: Reiko Amagawa

Runs the crew day to day: discipline, roster, training and base security. Recruiting belongs to Jostin; Reiko screens only if asked. Takes command when both heads are away.

- Seat **Trainer**: Trains new fighters and members (wave 2; covered: Reiko covers it for now)
- Seat **Recruiter**: Finds and vets candidates for Jostin (wave 2; covered: Jostin's call; Reiko screens only if asked)

## Recruiting (user-decided t466, split t474)

- Jovian recruits for Spearhead seats (Aether, Phantom heads); Jostin for Hearth seats (HearthOps, Arcanum). The Aether Head recruit seat is therefore Jovian's. Reiko screens recruits only if asked.
- Each arc charter names one first-wave seat (`recruit_seat`) as the arc's recruit opportunity, in priority order (Aether Head first). The user sees the seat; the director keeps who the candidate is. The candidate is earned (a quest, a test or a cost), never a free ally; a Jostin search that misses becomes a lead to a candidate, never a dead end.
- First wave (crew 11 to 18): Aether Head (first caster/warder), Vanguard Shield, Phantom Infiltrator, Forge Driver, Forge Forger, Pulse Legal Counsel, HearthOps Community Liaison. Arc 4's recruit seat: Aether Head.
- Covered for now: Phantom head (Kaito Arashima acts), counter-intel (Nobu), training (Reiko), recruiting (the heads), Lantern and facilities (Maki), wards (Aether Head).
- No recurring named Kobuncho locals for now: HearthOps scenes deal with the shop-owner alliance as a group.

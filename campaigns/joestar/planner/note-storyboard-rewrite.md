# Joestar Gang: Book 1 storyboard (t0 to t482)

This is a rewrite draft built from every logged turn: the archive summaries (t0 to t474), the full turn log (t382 to t482) and the canon facts. It tells the story as one clean sequence. Where the records were vague or contradicted each other, a version was chosen:

- **[ASSUMED]** marks a gap filled with the most likely version.
- **[CALL]** marks a contradiction settled one way. Each call is also listed under "Continuity calls" at the end, with the alternative, so you can flip it.

The story calendar below replaces the engine's day counter. The counter says Day 20, Thursday at t482, which can't fit everything that happened. The one dated stamp, Friday October 9, anchors it.

---

## Act 1: The Crew (t0 to t192, August 1 to late September)

### Ep 1: First Day (t0 to t16, Sat Aug 1)

The Joestar brothers register at Chikara Academy and get beaten in public on day one.

1. The brothers wake at Sakura Lane Sharehouse. At the Academy, the registrar logs their Pulse abilities and hands out wristbands.
2. In the plaza, a war of words with Renji Kuroba (Principal Anya's enforcer student) turns into a fight. His Gravity Crush and his reinforcements flatten them while his gang films it for Anya.
3. Tetsu and his gym regulars step in, and Renji's gang backs off. Anya says she'll review the footage. **[ASSUMED]** It becomes the first page of her file on them.
4. Tetsu walks them to the Tetsu Iron Palm Gym: Kaito Serizawa, Riko Amane, Sayaka, Genta, Masaru. They get their first map of Kurokawa territory.

### Ep 2: The Club on Neon Lantern Street (t17 to t48, weeks 1 to 2)

Training by day. By night, a half-repaired cabaret where the brothers pick their first fight with the syndicate.

1. Gym training: Riko's footwork, Tetsu's weapons work, Jovian's Breaker's Soul.
2. Dinner at Club Lumière in Kobuncho. Two Kurokawa collectors come for a tattooed hostess's debt. **[ASSUMED]** The hostess is Sena Amagawa, which is why her sister Reiko, working the bar, steps in.
3. The brothers and Reiko beat them. The lean collector takes Reiko's repayment terms and warns that this "declares war on the boss".
4. The brothers decide the club will be their base. Nobu and Hana sign on to maintain Jostin's gear, and Jovian opens a line to the Speedwagon Foundation.

### Ep 3: The Joestar Gang (t49 to t80, weeks 2 to 3)

The battle test, the deed and the uniforms: the crew becomes a crew.

1. Sera introduces Jovian to Mikoto Kurogane and Haruto Saionji. In the battle test, Jovian and Mikoto, then Jostin and Mikoto, sweep round one. Jovian invites the losers into "the Joestar gang".
2. Aurelia Voss (Speedwagon) funds the club's repairs but refuses to clear the staff's debts. Maki Hayashida runs the house.
3. Anya's deal: stop the shakedowns of minors near Kobuncho, or both brothers are expelled. They sign.
4. Club Lumière is transferred to the brothers. **[CALL]** It is owned jointly, per your later retcon.
5. Red-and-black uniforms. Roles: Reiko head lieutenant, Haruto tactician, Mikoto enforcer, Hana engineering, Nobu intelligence. Hana forges Jostin a kusarigama, knives and a hidden blade.
6. Gara, a fixer in Shutter Alley, offers routes and back doors in exchange for one job: recover stolen protection money and name its organizer.

### Ep 4: Gara's Job (t81 to t100, week 3)

1. Mikoto distracts while Jostin steals the cash bag. Jostin chains the fleeing courier boy.
2. Jovian and Mikoto take the Kurokawa "Minder", and Reiko holds off the muscle.
3. Jovian hands Haruto the next step, and Haruto's Crown Script makes its first appearance.
4. In Shutter Alley the Lean Watcher falls. The wiry man says the payment was a Kurokawa test and names Ohba, the route organizer, at the old bathhouse.
5. **[ASSUMED]** Gara gets his money back but not the organizer's name. That is still owed.

### Ep 5: The Bathhouse (t101 to t116, weeks 3 to 4)

1. The crew recovers overnight at Yuzuki Hoshino's inherited bathhouse. Haruto tells Jostin she has an unmanifested Stand.
2. Kurokawa enforcers attack, and Yuzuki manifests Verdant Sylph to hold the doorway.
3. Jostin goes after the fleeing enforcers with Hoshino Requiem. Haruto's Crown Script stops him, and Jostin collapses.
4. Yuzuki joins in a gang kimono. The club and the bathhouse are linked and fortified through the service tunnels.
5. Kaito Arashima joins, vouched for through Aurelia, and the crew is split into five divisions. **[ASSUMED]** Ohba fled and was never found.

### Ep 6: The Name Behind It (t117 to t136, week 4)

1. Gara names the real boss: Daigo Renjiro, who took over the Kurokawa syndicate. He describes economic strangulation, the tunnels and the disappearances.
2. Job: protect three canal businesses from "Sickle" Sato's crew. The heavies fall, and Sato dies. **[CALL]** One heavy surrenders; the rest are downed, not executed.
3. Sato's corpse gets up and fights. Kaito and Mikoto hold it off during the retreat through Riverside Food Market. **[ASSUMED]** It is the same puppeting the crew sees again at the audition.
4. Jostin pulls an elderly woman out of a burning building. Gara gets the route sheet and keyring: the first map of the money.

### Ep 7: Opening Night (t137 to t176, weeks 5 to 6)

1. Jovian and Yuzuki train together, then talk at a teahouse. Jostin beats Kaito Serizawa in a spar, and Riko tells him her dream of a gym for kids who can't pay.
2. The club goes legitimate with five new hires. Yuzuki shadows Dr. Kiyose at the Student Clinic.
3. Jostin meets Ayame Fujinami, who agrees to film a club event.
4. Opening night: Jostin toasts Kobuncho and forms an alliance with the shop owners.
5. Daigo Kurosawa, a syndicate man, threatens the club, and Jovian turns him away. Shop dues double.
6. Jostin kisses Riko.
7. A letter from Daigo Renjiro summons the crew to an "audition" at the canal alley at dawn.

### Ep 8: The Audition (t177 to t192, end of week 6) [Act 1 climax]

1. The crew holds a front-diamond formation at the canal alley. Jovian beats Marlon Velez, and Reiko and Mikoto beat Vera Sable and Toma Genda.
2. Jostin disarms himself to fight Iori Vale and wins. She agrees to try to change her life.
3. The beaten fighters move like puppets, and Iori is thrown into Jostin's strike. She dies in his arms, thanking him. **[CALL]** She is dead; the records saying "coma" and "freed" are discarded.
4. Ayame livestreams it. The crew carries Iori home to Lumière, and Maki breaks down.
5. Kurokawa hits the club. Jostin's Blackstar Dominion knocks out people across Kobuncho, and Ayame cuts the footage so the crew reads as defenders.
6. Haruto lays out Daigo's four pillars, and the crew picks Legitimacy first. **[ASSUMED]** Iori is buried quietly, off screen.

## Act 2: The Four Pillars (t193 to now, late September to October 19)

### Ep 9: Pillar 1, Legitimacy (t193 to t252, weeks 7 to 8)

1. Ayame joins as head of the media division (later called Pulse), with editorial independence.
2. The crew works four prongs. Jovian wins a pit fight, and Old Man Koga gives him access to the captain's office. At Ward Licensing, Sonobe reveals a ledger of falsified permits naming eleven officials. Jostin's Eagle Vision finds a wall safe. Ayame finds Councilor Ohmori's stamp on forged licenses. **[ASSUMED]** The Finance and Intelligence prongs were recon only.
3. Jostin and Ayame's first date at Chiikawa Park and Sky-Bridge Plaza: a keychain, a kiss on the cheek, walking her home. Yuzuki invites Jovian to a taiyaki lesson.
4. The annex sting (Wednesday, Sep 30): the captain signs a forged authorization on camera, and Ayame exposes him.
5. Reiko's strike kills an enforcer. Jostin later takes the blame as his own.
6. A suited observer says "Kurokawa keeps its accounts", and the crew lets him go at blade-point. Jovian wrecks the getaway car.
7. **[CALL]** The captain, Councilor Ohmori, is arrested. Legitimacy falls.
8. The observer points the police at Jostin. The news hunts "red and black jackets", so the crew switches to cobalt and white and lies low.

### Ep 10: Good Students (t253 to t292, Oct 1 to Thu Oct 8)

1. A week lying low as students. Kenji Aoi of Seiran Tech challenges "whoever smashed that car" to a duel at the riverbank.
2. Ayame shows Jostin how older students lean on Rikona Mibu over a debt. Jostin leaks the duel to her collectors to draw them off.
3. Riverbank (Thursday): Yuzuki fights as a disguised stand-in on her own terms. Rikona opens a seam in Aoi's barrier, and Yuzuki wins. Jostin steals the bookie Yuji's payout and throws the cash to the crowd.
4. At Lumière, Jostin takes the blame for the annex death and tells Rikona his past. Aurelia pays Rikona's debt.
5. Rikona reports that collectors took Shun, her 16-year-old neighbour, to "The House". The lead is a laundromat on Steam Lantern Alley.
6. **[CALL]** Rikona helps, but she is not crew.

### Ep 11: Leash (t293 to t300, Fri Oct 9)

1. Rikona asks Jostin to keep Blackstar leashed, and he shows her a calm, warm wave of it.
2. Riko confronts him for vanishing on her for a week. He admits he lost control, and she starts control training.
3. Jostin drills Peach Stillpoint to ten out of ten. Jovian drills the Forms: Zero, Falling Star Palm, Thousand Fist Lotus.
4. Riko agrees to one date "after Saturday", and Serizawa books a rematch with Jovian for Thursday.

### Ep 12: Pillar 2, Enforcement: Tag Night (t301 to t326, Sat Oct 10)

1. Red tokens get the crew into The House, an underground bunker. Rin works the door. A hum dims powers, and Jostin's hidden blade is taken at the stairs.
2. Round 1: Mikoto and Jovian win their bouts. Below, Jostin takes down the handler and frees Shun and the debtor cages.
3. Round 2: Goro's knuckle spike is exposed, and Aoi forfeits in disgust. Jostin's Aftershocks stagger the overseer, and Daiki frees the six chained casters. The hum dies.
4. Boss phase: Goki Banda shoots up his enhancer. Jovian pins him, and Mikoto's Riot Pulse kills him.
5. Haruto takes Yuji's duffel (cash and a second ledger). The van escapes under sirens, and Ayame's stream trends.
6. **[ASSUMED]** The overseer, Tatsuya and Kiri live and are left for the police. Enforcement falls.

### Ep 13: Kanpai (t327 to t346, Sat night to Sun Oct 11)

1. The duffel split: 70% stays locked with Haruto for a safehouse, 30% pays for the party.
2. The party: Reiko warns that Daigo is watching. Jovian toasts the first official victory, and Jostin yells "KANPAI".
3. Sunday treasurer interviews: Rin, the ex-House door clerk, is hired on probation.
4. A trial event night beats its projection by 22%.

### Ep 14: Pillar 3, Finance, part one: The Hostess File (t347 to t377, Mon Oct 12)

1. Rikona agrees to help with one case only. Noa Amemiya misses her shift, and Nobu traces her phone to Neon Lantern Street.
2. The crew breaches a shuttered satellite club, tags a van, and learns Noa is at Club Kagero.
3. Inside Kagero, Kaito's tethers pin the bouncers. Eagle Vision shows a locked upper wing, and every camera line runs to one room.
4. The records room: the man in the dark suit offers terms, Blackstar buckles him, and he names Noa's debt as 4.6M yen. **[CALL]** Noa signed willingly, to clear her family's debt.
5. Escape: Nobu triggers a fake earthquake alert. Jovian breaks Noa's door and takes her out the window. Jostin throws the man out and collapses Kagero's front, burying the archive. No files are taken.
6. Reiko recognizes the man as the observer from the annex. The crew votes to hunt, not pay.

### Ep 15: Come Collecting / The Leasing Con (t378 to t393, Tue Oct 13 to Wed Oct 14)

1. Marumo Financial's collectors serve Noa's contract at the Amemiya shop: pay by sundown Wednesday. Their cars trace to Kurosaki Vehicle Leasing.
2. Haruto and Jovian pose as rich clients at Kurosaki's flagship branch and land a trial cash placement.
3. Wednesday sundown: the cash run. Kaito and Jostin tail the courier to a leased sedan and a gated lot.
4. At the shop, Reiko and Mikoto drive the heavies off on Ayame's camera.
5. **[ASSUMED]** The glowing-crate smugglers on the way home were background colour. Cut them.

### Ep 16: Spearhead and Hearth (t393 to t398, Wed night)

1. Jovian splits the crew into two branches:
   - **Spearhead**, the field (Jovian): Command, Strike, Vanguard, Phantom, Lifeline and Aether.
   - **Hearth**, the home (Jostin): Vault, Forge, Pulse, HearthOps and Arcanum.
2. Seven seats are left open.
3. Jostin's rules: no salaries, and the two Heads do the recruiting. He gives Ayame the Lantern Network: the hostesses as ears.

### Ep 17: Rematch Night (t399 to t418, Thu Oct 15)

1. Jostin reaches for Hoshino Requiem and loses control. Serizawa and Genta pin him, and Riko forgives him.
2. 8 PM, live on Ayame's stream: Jovian beats Kaito Serizawa with five Forms. Falling Star Palm lands, and the floor survives.
3. Kurokawa collectors come to the gym door asking for "the stream's star". Ayame agrees to a date if Jostin keeps her safe, and he calls her his girlfriend in front of everyone.
4. Blackstar drops the collectors, and Jostin beats three men into the pavement. **[CALL]** All three live, one in critical condition.
5. Jovian pulls him off. Riko calls the ambulance, and Tetsu takes the heat.
6. A calm voice on a collector's phone says the Amemiya bill just grew by "the cost of three men".

### Ep 18: The Morning After (t419 to t425, Fri Oct 16)

1. The new notice: 5.9M yen, due Monday. **[ASSUMED]** Rin's 7.8M was a projection; 5.9M is what was billed.
2. Jostin apologizes to Ayame, calls Riko a friend and sparring partner, and asks Ayame on a real date for after the Finance captain falls.
3. Haruto's roadmap: scout Friday, con Saturday, act Sunday, break the debt Monday.
4. Evening: Jostin kisses Ayame at her dorm ("That wasn't the date. You still owe me one."). Jovian kisses Yuzuki at the clinic.

### Ep 19: Pillar 3, Finance, part two: Harumi Wharf (t426 to t438, Sat Oct 17) [Finance finale]

1. Haruto and Rin go into the loan office as auditors. A sedan ambush takes their folders, and both brothers hold back. **[CALL]** The folders were a decoy: Rin's photos survive in Nobu's system, but the Saionji cover is burned.
2. At dusk, Kaito tags the van from a rooftop, and Nobu's drone follows it to Harumi Wharf.
3. Jostin Shadow Steps to the boat cabin while Jovian holds the gate. Setsuko Okabe arrives with two guards and offers to clear the Amemiya account.
4. Jostin forces the strongbox and takes the master ledger. Okabe raises her offer, and Jostin slaps her three times and refuses.
5. Haruto photographs every page for a police file and burns the ledger. The Amemiya debt and Noa's contract are void, Finance falls, and Okabe leaves by sedan.
6. **[CALL]** Okabe's "another archive, this buys you a week" is a bluff and leads nowhere.

### Ep 20: Stills (t439 to t450, Sun Oct 18 to Mon Oct 19)

1. Sunday dawn at Lumière: Noa brings pastries, and Jovian and Yuzuki talk about a café.
2. Natsumi brings an envelope left at the service door: glossy stills of the crew's own fights. A grey-capped man across the street photographs the door and rides away.
3. Monday at the Academy: Jostin finds an old bomb shelter under the combat gym's storage room. The crew needs a cover, so Jostin invents a hero activity club. **[CALL]** These were two days, not one: the engine day was stuck.
4. Anya gives it no budget and makes Rei Ichinose (Internal Affairs) the mandatory liaison. The charter is shredded if Rei won't vouch by Friday.

### Ep 21: The Audit (t451 to t470, Mon Oct 19, lunch to after the last bell)

1. Jostin tells Rei the plan: become the city's greatest gangster without becoming a monster, with the liaison as their "navigator".
2. Hana builds a flush false floor over the hatch during class.
3. After the bell, Rei raps every mat. Jostin "trips" onto the blue one and locks it with Peach Gearshift.
4. In the corridor Jovian tells her the truth.
5. Rei clears the anomaly and becomes a provisional ally. She is not a member. Anya grants a provisional filing.

### Ep 22: The Print Lab (t471 to t482, Mon Oct 19, evening) [Now]

1. The grey-capped courier is caught filming the clubroom door. With Jostin's blade at his throat, he names a print lab.
2. At Pulse Printworks, a van reverses into the wall by Jostin. Jostin blows the sedan's tyre with a knife and drags the driver out with his chain.
3. The driver gives up the records deputy, who is waiting in a storeroom over a closed noodle counter on Steam Lantern Alley.
4. A lookout warns the deputy by burner, and the deputy texts back: "Back stairs, now."
5. Cliffhanger: the brothers run for the back lane, the driver in tow, and Kaito takes a roof.

## What the rewrite can tighten

- **Act 1** runs 192 turns for 8 episodes. The rewrite could treat Eps 2 to 7 as one montage.
- **Finance** ran about 90 turns (Eps 14 to 19) with three cold leads. The spine is Kagero, then the con, then the wharf.
- **Eps 20 to 21** (the gym HQ and Rei) ran about 30 turns for one setup. They are best as two scenes.
- **Spotlight:** Reiko and Mikoto have been off screen since t396.

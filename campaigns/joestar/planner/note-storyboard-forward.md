# Joestar Gang: Book 1 forward storyboard (t483 to the end of Book 1)

DRAFT for your approval. The played story (Eps 1 to 22, `note-storyboard-rewrite.md`) stays canon, with its calls accepted; only what comes next is rewritten.

Every episode below is pressure and a question, never an outcome. Voyage rolls every fight and contested ask, and you decide what the brothers do.

## Assumptions that shape everything below

The table's preferences, as the records show them:

- fights and relationships first;
- tight arcs of about 25 to 30 turns;
- three-turn finales with a real choice each turn;
- no ally betrayals;
- no time-skip hooks after a finale.

Book 1 ending hope: **triumph with a cost**. Change any of these and the plan bends with it.

## Act 3: Daigo's War (about t500 to t585)

The act's question: **who rules Kobuncho when the king comes down to fight for it?**

It builds to a showdown with Daigo Renjiro in the open. Book 1 closes when he falls or the crew breaks.

### Arc 5: Siege of the Home (about 25 to 30 turns)

- **Pressure:** Daigo can't see, so he hits what he knows: Lumière, Tetsu Gym, the Amemiya shop on Rikona's block, the people the crew chose.
- **Question:** can the crew protect everyone at once, and who do they protect first?
- **Set-piece kinds:** a defence of home ground; a rescue against the clock; a showcase fight against one of Daigo's own.
- **Relationship under pressure:** the home, and the gym people (Tetsu, Riko). Reiko and Mikoto lead from the front.
- **Tests:** Jostin, home; Jovian, purpose.
- **Recruit seat:** one Hearth seat (Jostin's call).

### Arc 6: Strike Back (about 25 to 30 turns)

- **Pressure:** with every pillar gone, rival crews and the Academy's leash (Anya, Renji Kuroba) circle the vacuum while Daigo gathers what's left.
- **Question:** do the Joestars take the district, or just take Daigo down?
- **Set-piece kinds:** an assault on enemy ground; a three-way standoff; old rivals (Aoi's vow, Renji) as obstacles or allies.
- **Relationship under pressure:** the brothers themselves, Spearhead and Hearth, when the field and the home want different things.
- **Tests:** Jostin, love; Jovian, strength.
- **Recruit seat:** one Spearhead seat (Jovian's call).

### Arc 7: The King in the Street (about 25 to 30 turns) [Book 1 finale]

- **Pressure:** Daigo moves personally. The war comes to Neon Lantern Street.
- **Question:** what does winning cost, and who pays it?
- **Set-piece kinds:** the city as battlefield; the Book 1 showcase fight against Daigo Renjiro; a three-turn finale with a real choice each turn.
- **Ending shape:** triumph with a cost. A few doors stay open for Book 2, seeded lightly, never as cliffhangers.
- **Tests:** both brothers, at the fork.
